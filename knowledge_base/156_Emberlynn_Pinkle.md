When she was

She had wavy shoulder-length hair in a long bob cut that was dyed in an ombre of light turquoise and pink with black roots, indicating that it was her natural hair color. She also had makeup which consists of light pink eyeshadow, and black lipstick and nail polish.

For clothing, Emberlynn wore an alternative/gothic style black hoodie with a pink hood, drawstrings and accents, and a pink and black plaid skirt. She also wore long black over-the-knee stockings with pink toes and heels, along with cuffs being decorated with cat faces.

She accessorized with a round lilac pastel and gold pendant, with the wings and pentacle being outlined with gold, that inexplicably protected her from actual Netherspawns.

Fallen Spawn

, Emberlynn retains her body shape and aesthetic, but now she takes the appearance of a light blue-furred cat-like Netherspawn with cat ears, a small hot pink nose, and a light pink tail with the tip being the same color as her fur.

She now sports more devilish qualities, such as a pair of small black horns atop of her head, small dragon-like wings, and a mouth with sharp teeth inside. Her hair is now colored in three shades of pink, and her eyes have cyan irises with hot-pink sclera and slit pupils, with eyelids colored in a darker shade of her fur, and an extra third eye on her forehead with an extra eyebrow.

Her clothing remains the same, but with the exception of a pink dripping paw design on the center of her hoodie, and the cuffs of her stockings now being decorated with Netherspawn-like faces.

Emberlynn is shown to be a quirky, obsessive stereotypical otaku pastel goth girl with a love for Japanese culture and a monster fetish, to such an extent that her room is covered with monster-lover memorabilia from wall to wall. As shown, Emberlynn possesses a very detached perception of reality and likely had difficulty separating it from her hyperactive imagination.

She has delusions and a lack of social skills, as shown where she had showcased multiple instances of not noticing social cues of others nor reading the mood of her surroundings. She has extreme delusions of grandeur, as shown where her immediate belief upon her room being broken into by a Netherspawn attempting to kill her was that he chose her to be his lover, acting dramatic and flirtatious from this perception.
