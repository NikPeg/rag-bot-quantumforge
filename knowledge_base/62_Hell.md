Despite its chaotic environment, Netherrealm has laws and rules of its own known as "Netherspawn law". These rules and laws are enforced by Netherspawn royalty, such as the Morningstar family, and The Seven Cardinal Vices, most notably Satan, the Sin of Fury. Netherspawns who break these laws will face trial and punishment.

Fallen are unable to leave The Hubris Ring.

Fallen can advertise media outside The Hubris Ring, however.

Lower-class Netherspawns, such as imps, cannot steal and/or use magical items from high-ranked Netherspawns (ex. Stellar' grimoire) for undocumented personal use, such as illegal travel to the human realm, even if the owner allows them to do so.

The only known exception of this law is the usage of Azmod'

Asmodean Crystals

, which can be used for documented use and allow legal travel to the human realm.

The assassination or assault of a member of Netherspawn royalty is illegal and severely punishable by law.

Betrayal from a member of Netherspawn royalty (ex. the Ars Goetia) is considered treason.

It is unknown whether a betraying royal could face a death penalty or will only receive a different punishment due to Netherrealm's elitist views and beliefs.

Netherspawns, such as succubi, are allowed to travel to Earth for work or other reasons as long as they do so legally and do not cause any trouble on Earth that causes any environmental or supernatural effects (ex. mutating a

into a giant monster).

Netherspawns are required to have a human disguise when they're on Earth, as implied with how Vera threatened to get I.M.P in trouble and how Luna reprimanded them for going to Earth without human disguises in "Spring Broken".

The only exception of this rule is on Halloween, where Netherspawns can travel to Earth without human disguises, as humans will believe they're in costumes.

As of now, it is unknown if Fallen can face consequence for breaking Netherspawn law, as Netherborn Netherspawns are, so far, the only Netherspawns shown to face consequence. It is also unknown if the exemption of Netherborn Netherspawns from the Exterminations fit as a Netherspawn law due to it being an agreement between both Celestia and Netherrealm.

A species of Netherspawns known as Reapers act as the law enforcement of Netherrealm. Additionally, Netherspawns of other species can work as Netherrealm officers alongside Reapers.

Certain laws that apply on Earth appear to apply in Netherrealm as well, as shown in The Avarice Ring in "Exes and Oohs", robbing a bank is illegal and can lead to jail time, though the specifics of jail time and the law in Netherrealm are unknown.

In an interview, Vivziepop explained that the Black Hatter is an inside joke from when she and her crew were trying to figure out what Netherrealm's version of Chistmas lore would be, stemming from how old black and white films would have villains in black hats and heroes in white hats.
