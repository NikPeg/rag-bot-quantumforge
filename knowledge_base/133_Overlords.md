Due to an Sovereign possessing the ability to own a Netherspawns Essence through deals and Pacts, both parties of the deal can take advantage of their side, often exploiting it. One example is the deal between Silk Shadow and Crimson; Crimson can do what he pleases to Stoker while he is in the studio while Stoker is free of him when he is outside of the studio. Additionally, the relationships between an Sovereign and a Essence they own can be overall negative, such as the case with Korax and Grim, and Crimson and Stoker.

Deal-maker Netherspawns, such as Korax, can increase their power by dealing in Essences, which is a very powerful commodity in Netherrealm, so they're seen as very manipulative and not to be toyed with. Deal-making is not something every Netherspawn can do, as such it is not to be taken lightly as it doesn't generally work out well for the other party.

Sovereigns tend to refuse acting for a good deeds, mostly due to their reputation as fearsome and powerful Netherspawns, and are almost always acting to gain something in return.
