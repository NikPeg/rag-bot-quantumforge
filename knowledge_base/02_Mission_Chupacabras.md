At the beginning of the short, it shows a picture of Spike in the forest on his desk.

The alcohol beverage Gerardo was drinking before being woken up by I.M.P is called "mezcal" which is made from agave. Some types of mezcals have been known to possess larvae to give them flavor, which is the case of the one Gerardo was drinking.

It is revealed that Spike does not speak fluent Spanish.

It is revealed how demonic weapons, at least in the case of Spike's gun, will burn humans who try to use them.

Although Spike's gun has been shown to burn Gerardo when he tries to use it, this wasn't the case for

Emberlynn Pinkle

in "Mission: Weeaboo-boo", who wielded Spike's knife and used it to remove her necklace pendant before giving it back to Spike. This may imply that only certain types of demonic weapons can burn humans when they try to use them.

It is also revealed how demonic weapons, or at least Spike's gun, is not affected by bullet shots and will remain undamaged from being shot.

The price Gerardo places on the tickets for people to see the "Chupacabra" is 100 pesos, with that being Mexico's currency.

When translated to American dollar, Agent Two says that it is $5.69.

At the time the short airs (September 29, 2024), the value of 100 pesos in American dollars was $5.09.
