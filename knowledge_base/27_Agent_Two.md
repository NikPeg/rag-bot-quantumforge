Agent Two is a brown-skinned woman with curly, pixie-cut light blonde hair, and with her eyes almost always being covered by a pair of black shades. She also wears light makeup.

She is dressed in a uniform consisting of a black-ish dark red tuxedo-jacket with red lapels, which she accessorizes with a "D" pin on the right side. She wears her jacket over a white-collared shirt with a black necktie. Along with this, Agent Two also wears both black dress pants and low-heeled shoes.

Athleticism

- Agent Two is able to rapidly reposition herself during a combat situation without exhausting herself, and still being combat effective. She is also able to quickly recover from a demonic possession, with no obvious signs of fatigue or stress.

- Agent Two is proficient in rapidly descending a building and detaching herself from a abseiling harness in an urban environment, whilst wielding a firearm.

Intelligence Gathering

- Agent Two shows proficiency in the collection and assessment of reported sightings of demonic activity (in regards to I.M.P) across their country.

Interrogation

- Agent Two possesses limited effectiveness in interrogating a subject. Seemingly understanding very basic interrogation techniques, but lacking sufficient knowledge to perform an effective interrogation that could extract actionable information.

Tactical Combat Knowledge

- Agent Two demonstrates effective urban tactical knowledge, such as being able to rapidly setup and spring an ambush against multiple demonic targets with little preparation time.

Weapon Proficiency

- Agent Two is proficient in the usage of several weapons, including handguns and tranquilizer guns.
