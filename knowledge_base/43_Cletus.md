Cletus is a small cherub who takes the appearance of a white-skinned human baby with short, light coral puffed hair, a small light pink nose, and large periwinkle eyes with no irises or pupils. He also has pale rosy cheeks.

His outlines, eyebrows, and eyelashes are colored in a shade of dark purple. He also sports a white halo hovering above his head, and a pair of periwinkle and white feathered wings on his back.

Cletus wears a pair of coral overalls with two white buttons over a mid-sleeve light yellow shirt with a white peter-pan collar, and periwinkle flats.
