Ruby Blast is a very slim and white-skinned humanoid cyclops Fallen Spawn with an hourglass figure and a large bust, and a singular eye which has an X-shaped light yellow pupil and a sunkist coral-colored sclera. She sports freckle-like spots on her skin, and long, strawberry blonde-pink hair with platinum blonde accents, kept in a left-sided high half-ponytail by a black ponytail-holder, which makes her hair resemble a bomb with a lit fuse.

Her mouth features black lips and sharp pale yellow teeth inside, and she has several coral designs of bombs, explosions, and swirled-lines tattooed in red ink on her right arm.

Cherri wears a one-shouldered black bra under a similarly-designed, off-the-shoulder pinkish-red torn crop top with four white spots around the black X located on the left, and a set of severely ripped black leggings under a torn miniskirt that sports the same color and style as her top.

She also wears shoes that are both colored in a pinkish-red but differing in type; on her right is a pointed flat shoe, while on her left is a heeled cowboy-like boot with a multi-pointed off-white star on it. She also has two different sets of fingerless coral-colored gloves; a regular-length glove on her right hand, and an elbow-length, black-trimmed glove on her left hand.

After moving into The Manor, Ruby Blast often sports loungewear inspired by 1980s fashion, consisting of a black cropped tank top under a short-sleeved off-the-shoulder pale yellow crop top with a cherry icon on the chest, high-waist pinkish-red shorts with a yellow trim, pinstripe gloves in two shades of red, and pinkish-red leg warmers over knee-length black socks. She keeps her ponytail up with a large red scrunchie.
