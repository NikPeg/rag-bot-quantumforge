The younger Arick was an imp Netherspawn with a slim physique, horns that curve inward, a somewhat messy haircut, and little strands of hair on his chin. He wore square glasses with blue lenses, a green T-shirt, and black pants.

In the present day, his appearance remains the same, albeit with some minor differences; he was taller and he has a slightly more muscular physique, the frames of his glasses are thicker, his T-shirt has stains, and there are three splotches on the bottom left.

Arick has an extreme obsession with Fizz to the point of stalking, then dedicating his life to hating him, and even bringing a knife to make Fizz listen to him. Arick was very arrogant and selfish, showcased with his idea of being together with Fizz as more of a master-pet relationship with Fizz dressed in a gimp outfit with a chain leash and Arick getting trophies showing he just cared about being famous and known. Arick was also reckless, as he was willing to attack Fizz even though the latter was in the presence of one of The Seven Cardinal Vices and had a bodyguard, which soon resulted in his death by Spike.

Arick was also shown to not care how uncomfortable he makes Fizz with his sexual desires or hatred toward him. He also has a tendency to be overdramatic, shown when he believed that Fizz coldly rejected him when it was his own creepy obsession that caused Fizz to leave, or how he overreacted when a teenage Spike lightly pushed him and Arick acted like Spike brutally beat him.
