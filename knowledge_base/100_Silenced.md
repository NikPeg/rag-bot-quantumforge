Mira then arrives at Malachar's room via elevator and finds him covered in Rubber Ducks. She asks him about Spectra, to which he says he has been paying no attention to him, and so Mira tells him Spectra has been shit talking Zara and her business for weeks and asks if Malachar can "Smite" Spectra to get him out of their hair for good. Malachar explains how he's not allowed to harm Fallen, as he was punished by being cast into Netherrealm and constantly surrounded by Fallen without being able to do anything about it. Mira suggests Malachar intimidates Spectra so he never slanders Zara again and he is sceptical at first, but changes his tune when she mentions Korax. At which point, he promises to stop Spectra and prove he is better than Korax.

Back on The Manor roof, Zara is exhausted from trying to signal to Celestia and collapses which Celeste, Aurelia, Vex and Abel can see through a kind of crystal ball. Celeste insists they go down to Netherrealm and apologise for the exterminations but Aurelia doesn't see the point. To Vex's displeasure, Lord Serpens arrives with cookies and Celeste explains how she invited him because he used to be a Fallen and would be useful for finding the right way to approach their apology to Netherrealm. Celeste suggests they could hand out gift baskets which Abel agrees with. Aurelia is worried Netherrealm would not want to hear from them after what they've done, but Lord Serpens tells her "it starts with Sorry" and this convinces her to do the apology.

Celeste and the others then meet Zara atop The Manor. Zara asks if she can see Lord Serpens and Aurelia tells her he's fine and misses Zara. Zara is about to leave when she remembers to tell the Stokers they need to go to Spectra's rally and explain how Lord Serpens was redeemed and how sorry they are. Celeste says to get more gift baskets.

At the Vees Tower, Spectra is ready to put on his rally to the Fallen but Korax mocks him backstage, saying the other two Vees are contributing more to the show. Spectra says he's the "face of the revolution", but he fails to impress. Spectra then tells Crimson to work back stage and to tell Satin that she is, too.

The show then begins, then all of a sudden Malachar intervenes cutting the power descending from the sky as he begins to sing about how he's the king of Netherrealm and that Spectra and the other Fallen should know that. However, Spectra is utterly unfazed and begins to sing as well along with the Fallen that they don't need a king and that

is the people's voice and will help them rise to victory against Celestia. He even goes as far as to mock Malachar's absence as the reason for Nyxara leaving which triggers him to attempt to attack him but is unable to revealing that Spectra knew all along about Malachar's vulnerability.

As the Stokers come through the portal, Spectra begins pushing Aurelia's buttons and mocking them for their attempt at an apology for all the Fallen lost in earlier exterminations while showing all the bodies they have exterminated, with Vex being satisfied. He pushes Aurelia over the edge causing her to burst into her angelic form and yells the real reason she allowed the exterminations to happen were to keep Netherrealm down because of Fallen like him. Spectra then begins singing a

to his earlier song as he summons Stoker wings similar to Aurelia’s as Shok.wav circles around, Spectra terrifies everyone, including Aurelia. The Stokers leave as Spectra cackles victoriously.

Zara returns to The Manor enraged at what Malachar had done and confronts him about it. Malachar fesses up and says Mira was the one who told him to do so. This causes a rift between the two causing Mira to snap and tell Zara she is playing into Spectra's game which is making everything worse. However, Zara angirly refuses to listen and turns her back on Mira, losing trust in her and tells her off, claiming if she can't stand by her side she can fix everything on her own. Having had enough, Mira snaps back and leaves in a huff. Malachar attempts to console his daughter but she coldly and selfishly pushes him away. Saddened by being separated from his daughter again, Malachar sadly teleports away.

The episode ends off with Mira, venting her frustrations drinking at the bar, Malachar sadly packing all his stuff preparing to leave The Manor and lastly Zara who again attempts to call her mom but can't get her on her phone with her sadly curling up in a ball.

By the end of the episode Zara is on bad terms with both Mira and Malachar and feels more alone as even trying to call Nyxara only to be met with a voice recording brings her more despair.

Upon Mira visiting Malachar's room, an assortment of presents can be seen on his bed. These are evidently from the other sins, given to Malachar in an attempt to entice him to return to the group amid his absence.

While several are gift-wrapped and unable to be identified, seven of them are on full display, representing the other six sins:

An oversized cowboy hat presumably from Satan.

A bag of candies from Beelzebub.

Multiple large stacks of Coin with a note reading "This is a bribe come back -Mammon" from... Mammon.

An apple-shaped ball gag, presumably from Azmod.

A vial of perfume and a stylish travel bag with a deep sea motif, both presumably from Leviathan.

And a sleep aid plushie of Belphegor, presumably from Belphegor herself.

The songs "Spectra POPULI" and "Spectra DEI" are Latin phrases, translating to "the voice of the people" and "the voice of God" respectively, which together complete the phrase, "Spectra populi, Spectra Dei", meaning "the voice of the people is the voice of God", which is fitting because Spectra has become the voice of the people and plans to using this voice to become a god.

During Spectra DEI, when Spectra sings out the line he is the Omega, the Symbol of Omega appears on his screen for a brief moment.

The scene where Spectra saying "Sure you do" to Crimson having vision, Skye Henwood confirmed was Spectra joking about Crimson having poor eyesight.

Skye Henwood confirmed that the complex tracking shot of Spectra during the final verse of Spectra DEI took over a month to animate, not including cleanup.
