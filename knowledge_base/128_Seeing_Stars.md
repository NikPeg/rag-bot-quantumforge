, with a highly territorial clown and religious protesters (among others) making it harder for her to get around. She runs into a corner to hide from people as she panics. Spike, also panicking, has no idea how to tell Stellar about the situation until some time later, he does reach him on the phone. Thinking the situation went well, Spike relaxes, but then Stellar, in his full demonic form, barges in looking visibly furious. Meanwhile, Octavia struggles to get around, asking people for help in how she can see the stars, with no success. When she finds a pamphlet with the words "Starstruck Tourz" on it, she figures she can find what she's looking for there. Back to a frantic Stellar, he is trying to figure out why and where Octavia left, and how they would find her. Luna, using her scent, deduces that Octavia is somewhere in

Los Angeles

As Stellar and the rest of I.M.P enter a portal to Los Angeles, both Stellar and Luna transform into their human forms. Without his grimoire, Stellar can't use his powers to give the Imps human disguises. Spike questions Stellar for not remembering his spells, but he dismisses him and implies Spike does not have a better memory than him. As the Imps follow from behind, Mox and Mill are stopped by a rapper trying to promote his music. Mox realizes he needs money to pay him for his music, despite Mill's opinion on how pointless this may be. When a stranger gives Mox a coin thinking he's in costume, he gets an idea. After a bus tour, Octavia realizes her desire to look for the stars was misdirected.

At the same time, Spike exits a costume shop, finding what he can to disguise himself. It works all too well when he is mistaken for an actor named Brennon Ragers. Meanwhile, Mox and Mill sing a brief

, which catches the attention of several pedestrians and tourists and makes them enough money to pay the rapper, who jealously tells them to leave. However, Mox is immediately distracted once again by a stand selling souvenirs. As fans pile on top of "Brennon Ragers", asking for autographs and selfies, a TV crew tries to take him for a show that Brennon Ragers was supposed to guest star in. Spike immediately refuses and attempts to escape, but his attempts remain futile as he is thrown into a truck. As a crowd fights over his false ears, Stellar, posing as Brennon's agent, is also thrown in the truck. Spike, still seeing hope, breaks the window and advises Luna to look for Octavia.

With the tour over, a visibly annoyed Octavia rips the pamphlet in half and continues to wander around the city, ignoring the things around her, until she passes nearby a souvenir shop. She takes a picture of the location, as well as check out the inside. Back to a protesting Spike, he's expected to perform in front of an audience in a sitcom while reading lines off a teleprompter without any time to rehearse, which visibly haunts him from the last time he ever performed. Stellar reassures him that he'll do a great job and throws him onto the stage. Spike speaks his lines given via the aforementioned teleprompter, to which the audience laughs, boosting his confidence. At that point, he decides to keep playing along with the show.

Luna wanders around the city with a hot drink, spotting the same souvenir shop Octavia was at minutes ago. She takes a picture and posts it on her Sinstagram and as she scrolls down, she notices Octavia took a picture of the same location she was at. She then looks at some of Octavia's recent posts of her at random locations in Los Angeles. For every picture she's in, Luna tries to find her at that same location, but to no avail. She stops at an observatory, but keeps on looking for her. Meanwhile, at Starstruck Studios, Spike is still going along with the acting. When he's expected to play a scene where a family adopts Uggie, this reminds him of the moment, as the scene flashback, when he had first met Luna at the adoption center. Seeing the depressing condition Luna was in, Spike takes pity upon her. The scene cuts back to the present, on set. Spike, in a traumatized rage, refuses to give Uggie to the acting family. This leads to an argument that escalates to the point where Spike breaks character and tries to kill everyone on set. Stellar tries to help Spike by throwing a bottle of LA water at the director, which burns him and makes him knock over the teleprompter, killing the lights momentarily and setting the studio ablaze. Spike then grabs Stellar's hand to escape the ensuing chaos and search for their daughters.

Back at the observatory, Luna is still looking for Octavia until she finally spots her on top of a ledge, despondent and alone. Octavia expresses disappointment that all of her efforts to see the meteor shower have reached dead ends, and that the night sky is overall covered by smog thanks to the city below. This gives the two a moment together, as Luna, who reverts back in her normal form, talks to her about how Stellar is looking for her. However, she doesn't believe this, as Luna is the one who found her and he wasn't. She is visibly upset that Stellar was so caught up about hating Stella that he had forgotten about his promise to her, asking why that is the case. Luna gives this some thought and tells Octavia that relationships with fathers can be difficult, but they always try their best to be there for their children. Luna reveals that Stellar is somewhere in the city looking for Octavia. She returns the grimoire to Luna and thanks her with a hug, ready to return to Stellar.

As Spike and Stellar search for their daughters, Luna and Octavia teleport to them via portal. Spike tries to hug Luna and apologize for even considering replacing her, only for her to kick him in the crotch. Stellar embraces Octavia, relieved that she is okay, then questions why she ran off. Octavia reminds him about his promise to take her to see Azathoth's tears. Having been caught up in moving Stella out, Stellar had forgotten all about it. Devastated and ashamed, he apologizes to his daughter, who forgives him and says she's glad he's here now. Suddenly, fireworks shoot up in the sky and both fathers and daughters take the time to enjoy the moment. Meanwhile, Mox and Mill are ready to head back by entering a portal to I.M.P headquarters. Mox, having collected many souvenirs, is stopped by another rapper, but Mill, by this point fed up with Mox's naivety, kills the salesman and carries Mox through the portal.
