After confirming to everyone in Netherrealm that Reformation is possible, and finally achieving peace between the realms, Celeste is now actively helping with The Manor, constantly visiting and sending gifts from Celestia as well as being in charge of welcoming any future redeemed Essences who ascend to Celestia.

In her humanoid form, Celeste takes the appearance of a tall and slim young woman with silver-like skin, black lips, and mid-thigh-length hair with bangs styled in a somewhat messy way, colored in a gradient of periwinkle with noticeable white roots.

Her eyes have light blue sclera with white-outlined darker-colored pupils and bushy, dark indigo eyelashes, and her eyelids are perano-colored, while her three freckles are white and are located on each of her cheeks and her nose. She also has six white feathered wings on her back, however as of “

Curtain Call

” her middle left wing was maimed by the Might of Nyxara, and was subsequently replaced by a golden cybernetic prosthetic. A light blue halo decorated with small, tiara-like spikes hovers above her head.

Celeste wears princess-inspired clothing which consists of a light periwinkle gown with a darker-colored stripe around the high-frilled turtleneck collar, darker-colored trimming and details on the short light blue puffed sleeves, a darker-blue star-pattern on the chest, and darker-blue triangular-like patterns on the hem with small diamond shaped between the mounds. In addition, she accessorizes with a light blue sash worn around her thin waist, and wears a pair of white tights underneath.

She also wears a set of long fingerless, periwinkle-gray opera gloves which covers most of her arms, which appears to be skin-tight and act as long sleeves for her dress. For footwear, she wore blue ballerina flats.

In her angelic form, Celeste’s skin becomes white, her head becomes oval-shaped with an extra third eye on her forehead, her lips are noticeably paler, and she becomes bald. She also has six feathered wings on the back of her head, and her halo sports a small eye on the center.

Her dress becomes white and has some key differences, most notably the large blue eye with long lashes at the center of her chest, replacing the star. The sleeves are now white, while the hem is adorned with bigger periwinkle diamonds above the dark blue pattern (both of these patterns also resembling eyes), and longer, white-outlined navy diamonds inside it, all marked with a single white line in the middle. She also has a white feathery neckline and petticoat.

Celeste is excitable, bubbly, sweet, friendly and kind to almost everyone around her. She does not discriminate others based on mistakes or who they are, where she excitedly greeted Zara and welcomed her to Celestia, despite her being a Netherspawn. She compliments Aurelia in personality, with Aurelia the most serious and Celeste the more bubbly. Working alongside Aurelia, her job is to preserve the happiness and joy of the human Essences that ascend to Celestia.

Celeste also holds a great amount of sympathy towards human Essences and sees them all as equal, much like Zara, as she is disgusted and horrified upon learning about The Purge.

She is also shown to engage deeply in the things that she believes in, such as when she promises Zara to find a way to help her. As shown in "
