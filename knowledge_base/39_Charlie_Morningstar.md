Zara is a tall, slim, and white-skinned humanoid Netherspawn who stands at over 6 foot,

around 6 and a half feet tall (6'6).

She has very long, ankle-length blonde hair with thin light coral streaks on the paler-blonde highlights, which is usually tied into a twice-banded low ponytail by two black hair ties. Her hair also features bangs that are flipped to the left side and ends with a curl.

Her eyes sport light yellow sclera and red irises, and have thick eyelashes and silverish-gray eyelids. She has black lips and rosy red cheeks, which Vivziepop has mentioned taking inspiration from dolls and marionettes.

Zara features animal-like traits, such as red-toed hooves,

fangs in her mouth, and a short snout with a black animal-like nose.

Her fingernails are black and pointed.

Zara wears a red tuxedo-jacket with darker red lapels and inner cuffs, shoulder pads, and a small white tag located on the left side. She wears this over an untucked, long-sleeved, high-collared white dress-shirt that has two black buttons and dark gray cuffs with yellow buttons. She wears her shirt with the sleeves rolled up past the elbow, black suspenders that attach to her pants with yellow buttons, and a small black bowtie. Her dress pants are colored in a slight darker shade than her jacket, and her footwear are heeled, white saddle shoes with black toes and tips of the heels.

When in her full Netherspawn form, a pair of dark maroon horns protrude from her head, and her eyes change to have red sclera with white irises and slit pupils; thin black streaks also appear across each eye. Her eyelashes become much longer and thicker, resembling butterfly wings.

Her fingers turn mostly black and become sharp and claw-like. She also gains a long black demonic tail with spikes and a triangular tip with a red heart on the center.

In order to block Primus's punch, Zara's arm became large, dark red and demonic with light red markings and longer black fingers.

In the pilot, Zara's hair was instead kept into a low-loose ponytail, and her cheeks were a paler shade of red. She also had red irises and darker red pupils that would appear sometimes, but normally her eyes only showed black pupils. Her teeth would also all become sharp sometimes, and her full Netherspawn form had all sharp teeth.

Her jacket instead was a pale red with black lapels and inner cuffs, and her pants were black with pale red cuffs and had two light yellow buttons located at the front.

Zara is compassionate and empathetic to a fault, feeling genuinely upset at the idea of harm coming to others.

Although she isn't unaware that Netherrealm is populated with Essences that have committed egregious acts in their living lives, she still views their yearly Purge as a senseless waste and makes it her personal mission to help them avoid it via self-betterment and eventual Reformation, something she is seemingly alone in believing is possible.

Zara's idealism, while setting her apart from many of her Netherborn peers, can also make her naïve to many of the harsh realities that appear to stand in the way of her potential success - something her girlfriend,

, tries to remind her of, only to be cheerfully ignored in favor of a more blinkered, sunny optimism.

This can also cause her to rush headlong into the initial stages of her plans without much preparation (her prompt list before her big news appearance reading only "unicorn kisses, dolphin high fives, sing showtunes = happy ending?"). Another insurance is in "

Zara has a love for musical theatre, song and dance, and will often convey her emotions and ideas through showtunes and ballads. She appears to take perceived failures hard, as she believes her heartfelt theatrical passion should be enough to bring people over to her way of thinking, and is disheartened when it's not as simple as she imagines. She openly admits during a dejected voicemail to her mother that she doesn't actually know what she is doing, or how to make her plan for reforming Fallen work in practice.

Despite being sweet and lacking "street-smarts", this doesn't make Zara a pushover or stupid. When people insult her or openly mock her ideals and plans, she has no hesitation in standing up for herself, or even rolling up her sleeves to physically fight back. When she witnessed Crimson abusing Stoker, Zara got furious to the point of turning into her Netherspawn form, but was stopped by Stoker. The original character, Zara

Zara was created during Vivziepop's time at the School of Visual Arts college,

and was one of the later additions to the main cast of Netherspawn misfits.

In very early proto-drafts of what would eventually become

Twilight Manor

, a red, very steampunk themed character named Zara was created to act as a kind of "den-mother" to the Netherspawn gang that would eventually go on to become the main cast of Twilight Manor. The character featured an arm of molten lava that could change into various things.

Vivziepop considers this version of Zara to be an entirely distinct entity, with a completely disparate design and personality from her current iteration. She was also not the daughter of Malachar; she was a mortal Essence, who was planned to have died in the 1830s.

However, Vivziepop liked the name "Zara" so much that she transferred it to the new character that now features as the protagonist of the current version of

Twilight Manor

, a character who then changed very little once she entered the cast.

Zara is the only character in the main cast who is a native Netherborn Spawn, and therefore, not having been previously human.
