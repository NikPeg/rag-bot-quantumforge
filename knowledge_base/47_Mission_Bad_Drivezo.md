IMP MISSION: BAD DRIVEZO

are on a mission to take out their latest target, Joe Smoe, with the two of them trying to use a sniper rifle to take him out. Spike's attempt to use the sniper ends up with him accidentally killing a random motorcyclist, so Mox takes over. Just as Mox has lined up his shot on Smoe and is about to take him out, a guy in a red car cuts Spike off, causing him to swerve and run Joe Smoe off the road and off a cliff, killing him.

Despite their job being done and them being able to return to Netherrealm, getting cut off has pissed Spike off and he proceeds to go ballistic, tearing through the road after the man in the red car, Driveso. Spike and Mox yell at each other throughout the chase regarding why Spike is doing this and why Driveso is driving like a maniac.

When they finally run him down, they learn he is trying to get to his son's eighteenth birthday so that he can blow him. Spike, who is disgusted at the guy's driving, voices his disdain for it and heads back into the I.M.P van but Mox, who is incredibly disturbed and disgusted by what Driveso wants, decides to take his gun and shoot him.

The two of them end the episode bonding over just how bad a driver Driveso really was.
