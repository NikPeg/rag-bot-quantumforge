The meter of "Frozen Palace" is in three and waltz like. Stellar' theme is also in three quarters, and later when we hear Vassago's theme it is also in three quarters. Brian Sadler tried to keep all of the themes for the Goetic Princes in this meter while each having their own distinct sound. As Andrealphus gets more agitated, the music also starts to get more agitated as well.

"Office Escape" had another request from Vivziepop to have music get a little heart wrenching as Luna is reluctant to destroy her sentimental belongings. This cue ends with some hard rock guitars and stops suddenly when the team crashes into a post.

"Captured/Proceedings" is the introduction to Satan, but he didn't have a theme yet as there really wasn't enough time for Brian Sadler to develop something for him, and with all the dialogue, Sadler wanted the music to remain in the background and not be too distracting. There is a general feeling of evil with rising and falling chord progressions and dark choir. Andrealphus' theme is also more evil than in the beginning, but still a little light and Christmasy as he dashes around the courtroom.
