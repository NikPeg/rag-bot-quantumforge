Clara is a tall and slim humanoid demoness with puce skin, curly cream hair tied up in a high ponytail, black lips, eyes with bright red sclera and white irises, and small black horns atop her head.

She wears a sleeveless black turtleneck crop top, gray jean-shorts with a black belt, black fingerless gloves, long white over-the-knee stockings and black ballet shoes like her mother.
