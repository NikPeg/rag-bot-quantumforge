Little is known about this mafia organization. The majority of its members consist of an unknown species of

Netherborn Netherspawns

with shark-like appearances, snake-like appearances, dinosaur-like appearances, imps, a smaller contingent of hybrid Netherspawns and many unknown Netherborn Netherspawns. This mafia has shown itself to be unquestionably loyal to its boss, Crimson, prepared to kill whomever crosses him, or accept any orders without question.

The extent or type of criminal operations this organization partakes in is unknown, its members have been witnessed brutally torturing and murdering other imps and Netherspawns for unknown reasons. Mox, a former member, was witnessed performing a bank heist in the past, however it is unknown if this mission was sanctioned by the organization or if it was a rogue operation.

Crimson mentions in "Exes and Oohs" that the organization is experiencing significant financial stressors that has forced it to find alternative sources of funding, leading them to attempting to force Mox to marry

Chazwick Thurman

in order for him to join their mafia and inherit his (alleged) fortune.
