Rings of Netherrealm

The Seven Cardinal Vices represent the law and order of Netherrealm, tasked with keeping the affairs of the Netherspawn hierarchy in a controlled system. Its members consist of powerful, high-ranking Netherspawns with abilities grand enough to influence the environment and entities of Netherrealm itself. These Netherspawns hold a position of influence in the rings that represent their respective sin, with themselves overseeing the operations of the ring and their respective populace. Because of this, the matters of the sins are relatively isolated from each other. However, the sins will come together during a time when a violation concerning Netherrealm's laws and the established hierarchy takes place, and collectively carry out the judgment.

One member is designated as the leader of the other sins, typically by influence and power. The order was founded by Malachar Morningstar, a

Fallen Stoker

who served for a time as both the Deadly Sins' leader and the ruler of all of Netherrealm. However, with Malachar's absence,

took advantage of his desertion to subsume control, thereby becoming the effective ruler of Netherrealm, with the Deadly Sins forced to carry on without Malachar. Although the members are of equal footing outside of their leader, this does not indicate members often respect each other; internal conflict can happen between Deadly Sins, as is the case with

, the latter of whom once threatened the former.

The Deadly Sins are strongly associated with the species and inhabitants of the rings they hail from. The Deadly Sins themselves generally serve in the interest of upholding Netherrealm's power hierarchy, as such they are derogatory toward weaker Netherspawns, although can make occasional exceptions. Like most of Netherrealm's elitist mindset, intermingling between these Netherspawns and lower-class Netherspawns are looked down upon, considering it a disgrace against the natural order of Netherrealm's society.
