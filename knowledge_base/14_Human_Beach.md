human beach

is the tentative name of a beach located in the United States of the

living world

that appeared during the second half of "

Spring Broken

The beach is a tropical shore off an unspecified city somewhere in the United States. The sea water is light blue, and the waves are medium level.

Adjacent to the beach is a wall that divides it from the town, in which several stores and places are located. There is a wooden flight of stairs intersecting this wall, and nearby is a row of wooden docks.

There also appears to be signs of wildlife in the neighboring ocean, as shown by the

unsuspecting catfish

that was swimming there.

It could be assumed that the beach is located somewhere in the American state of Florida, as shown on the schedule of the real-life official "

Netherrealm Tour" shirt from Shark Robot.

One of the stores located on the street overlooking the beach is named as "Dagon's Juice".

The storefront where Spike hangs the discount flyer is labeled "Erotic Cakes", a reference to the same-named bakery featured in

The Simpsons

Treehouse of Horror VI

The billboard seen that says "NOTICE ME, SENPAI (T^T)" is a reference to the meme derived from the Japanese media trope of a character infatuated with someone they consider their senpai.

On the Prime Video version, the billboard has been changed to a picture with silhouettes of trees.
