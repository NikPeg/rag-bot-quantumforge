As of this episode Mira has regrown her wings, and the residents of The Manor are now aware that she is an Stoker.

This also implies Stokers may have the ability to regenerate their wings.

Before the episode aired, Amazon Prime had accidentally released a screenshot of Mira with her wings as part of a series of sneak peek material, which was then immediately removed.

Steelheart reveals that she's already aware that Mira was an Cleanser based on the latter's x eyepatch and her angelic spear.

Additionally, she also reveals that Stokers can also be killed by their own weapons.

Carmilla is revealed to have Stoker Steel ballet shoes, of which she reveals she used in order to successfully kill an Cleanser.

Susan, as well as the "Shrunken Head Keychain", are listed as among the official trading cards that can be collected in the packs sold by Amazon.

According to Vivziepop, the entire concept of Susan was a after-the-fact alteration "for reasons". She added that writing Korax beefing with a random old lady was fun too.

Grace teasing about how Zara was Korax's girlfriend before saying he's "an ace in the hole" is a reference to how despite Korax being aromantic asexual, he is not aware of the modern labels.

When Grace first grabs him, Korax bleats like a fawn, which is a sign of happiness and contentment for deer.

Zara and Korax finally make a deal, in which Zara got information about The Cleansers in exchange for a favor for Korax at a time of his choosing that harms no one.

Zara revealed when she was venting to Korax that she and Mira have been together for three years.

This is the first time Zara is shown to have goat-like hooves for feet.

It is revealed that Grace is the leader of the people of Feast District, and that the citizens will only do something if everyone agree to it.

Background designer Ashleigh Beevers revealed that the bass surrounded by a fence in Grace's room is based off how during production Ashleigh had to put a cage around her real-life bass because of her dog, to which Sam Miller found it funny and requested for a fence to be put in the show.
