Mox enters Vera's office to try to reason with her and her fellow

Netherspawn crew, but they take on full demonic forms to sexually assault him. Mox just barely escapes but is visibly hurt and covered in lipstick and collapses on the floor. Refusing to stand for his employees being assaulted, Spike furiously storms into Vera's office to challenge her. Since Spring Break in the mortal world is prime time for crime, he bets that I.M.P can kill more targets than the succubi can have sex with by the end of the day - a challenge Vera accepts.

Cutting to I.M.P's meeting room, Spike relays his somewhat cartoonish plan, which consists of finding lots of clients, taking the portal up to the mortal world and going on a murderous rampage, piling the corpses into a canoe, lighting it on fire before pushing it out to sea for sharks and maybe a goose to eat, and win the bet which they will rub in Vera's face.

Spike asks if anyone has questions, and Mox remarks that his description of the plan is nothing but a nonsensical wish list, which Spike brushes off as not being a question before the two start quarreling, taking turns insulting one another. Luna then interrupts them to ask if she can join them on this job. However, Spike initially forbids the idea, saying Spring Break is no place for "vulnerable goth girls", reminding her of all the human freaks who would drool all over her - leading to a fourth wall break where everyone looks at the camera with accusatory glares. Luna then points out that she can blend in with the humans since she has a human disguise and this surprises everyone, leaving Luna to angrily realize they've been operating in the mortal world undisguised the whole time.

Before she can say anything more, Spike develops a new plan where Luna lures targets into traps where they kill them. While they actually have the makings of a plan which Mill likes at this point, Mox then points out the biggest hole in the grand scheme: they need at least one client who would be willing to Pact enough kills to win the bet, reminding everybody that they can't just go on a slaughter fest. Spike states he has it covered, and the next scene cut reveals that it's a single flyer on a telephone pole, advertising an extreme 50% discount for kills at Spring Break. Mox complains that "there is no way [they] will get enough clients by the end of the day with one poorly spelled, bad grammar flyer". Just then, Mox is proven horribly wrong when a crowd stretching across the street forms a line in front of it. Spike elbows Mox about the plan's success before asking the crowd who is first.

It then cuts to the mortal world, where I.M.P hides under a pier on a beach with a Spring Break party. Spike goes over a few last-minute details of the plan, such as how they can't be seen or risk causing a panic with loose gunfire, and Luna assumes her magical human disguise. Spike comments that Luna looks "awful" disguised (however, his tone suggests the opposite) and tells her to go to work. A montage of murders with Luna luring targets into side alleys for the others to kill is then shown alongside other kills without her help. As Spike ties the knot on a bag with a corpse in it, Vera dramatically arrives, taking over a concert stage to put on a show in human disguise, a persona she apparently adopts often enough that the crowd is familiar with her.

As Vera sings a

hypnotically seductive song

, her fellow disguised succubi initiate an orgy with people in the crowd. Spike angrily comments about the song being a human mating call and that they must speed up if they want to win. He asks Luna if a beachgoer vomiting near them is on their list, and Luna, distracted by Whirl, mumbles that he is. Spike goes to kill him, but his victim turns around and mistakes him for a leprechaun, to which Spike jokingly agrees, "Yeah, pretty cool, huh?" before splitting his head open with an axe. As he turns to ask Luna to find the next target, he realizes she has disappeared. Panicked and asking where his "baby" has gone, Mill sees that she has gone to talk to Whirl, disguised and acting as stage security for Vera, causing Spike to become furious and protective.

Making her way through the crowd, Luna goes to talk with Whirl, but he knocks out a member of the crowd who attempts to rush the stage and drags them away. As Luna goes to follow him, one of the disguised Desire Netherspawns named Josh tries to seduce her but she ends his pursuit by punching him in the face. Vera then tosses a hipflask full of

Netherrealm-made alcohol

into the crowd but it bounces into the ocean where it begins demonically mutating a fish into a monster. Luna finally reaches Whirl, awkwardly introducing herself and Whirl recognizes her as working for Spike; Luna apologizes for that in case it's weird to him and he nonchalantly says it's cool since he isn't paid enough to care about or get involved in Vera's feud. Luna awkwardly flirts with him before Spike interrupts them and argues with Whirl. Mox complains to Mill that Spike has been distracted from the mission, and they conclude that it's up to them to finish the hit list, to Mill's excitement. They leave the beach to kill targets inside the buildings along the waterfront in a blood-drenched massacre.

Luna tells Spike to leave before he gets

the Netherspawns caught, but Spike asks her to get back to work and Luna asks if she isn't entitled to breaks. Spike yells, "We have a parking spot on the line!" Whirl tries to calm him down, and he rudely snaps at him to stay out of it. Luna asks if he can leave her alone for five minutes; Spike responds by demanding respect from her as her adoptive father. At this point, Luna then asks what matters, saying that Spike isn't her real father; she was almost eighteen when he adopted her. Spike says that it counts anyway, which Luna dismisses, telling him she didn't need him then and doesn't need him now.

Mill and Mox hide on a table of kegs and prepare to take out a target with a crossbow but a drunk partygoer then flips the table, sending them flying into the crowd. Mox is then mistaken for a possum by the drunken crowd and stuffed into one of the kegs, where he swallows large portions of the keg. As the crowd with the keg rushes past, Luna feels guilty and tries to apologize to Spike for her outburst. Spike halfheartedly tells her to enjoy her break and that he will "kill something".

Whirl then asks if Luna is alright as what she did was "savage" but compliments her for sticking up for herself, which makes Luna flush. Mill, who has found the keg Mox was stuffed into, abandoned by the crowd. Tipping it over, a drunk Mox spills out with what little's left of the beer. Mox drunkenly asks Mill where she got four heads and that he wants to kiss them. The now

mutated fish

with the hipflask spilled on it rises from the ocean with legs now about the size of a three-story building. The fish makes easy prey of the drunken and carefree Mox, grabbing him with its tongue and seemingly swallowing him whole. Mill then stabs a partygoer and takes his towel and drink, using them to make a Molotov, which she throws at the monster fish, causing it to stumble and fall.

The monster fish falls into the surf, and Mill swims out to it, using her knife to climb up to its mouth, which she pries open. There, she finds Mox clinging to its uvula to avoid being swallowed but he merely responds with a confused high-five as she reaches for his hand. She manages to grab him by the wrist and cuts him free from its tongue. Mox gets tossed out, and Spike catches him back on the beach. Still in the monster catfish's mouth, Mill brings it to spit her out by punching its wounded tongue before clambering to its face. Mox drunkenly declares his love for Mill to Spike, who mockingly remarks that she pegs Mox. Mill uses the monster's movement to springboard herself into the air, diving back into the monster's mouth and into its throat, then slashes it open from the inside, killing it.

Mill swims back to shore, asking Spike if Mox is OK. Spike tells her he's fine, before dropping him and Mill picks him up. They are then approached by Vera and the other succubi. Vera attempts to reprimand I.M.P for their lack of subtlety. Mill then tosses Vera her hipflask, telling her how bad it would look if they revealed she was behind the monster fish in the first place, to Mox's amusement. Vera then points out they would also get in trouble for being undisguised when Mox drunkenly hisses he was mistaken for a possum, which excuses them in that regard this one time. Spike then says he won't mention her involvement in the monster fish if they let him use the parking space, which Vera begrudgingly agrees to. Spike and Mill begin celebrating and rubbing their victory in Vera's face. The succubus calls to Whirl that they're leaving. Whirl tells Luna that he's got to go but gives her his number, saying his girlfriend throws great parties. Luna becomes disappointed upon hearing Whirl mention that he already has a girlfriend, seemingly shutting down her hopes of pursuing him romantically. However, she still seems willing to go along with his offer of friendship and any party invitation.

Spike then tells Luna to come over so they can celebrate their victory by parking the car in their space, and the Imps and Hellhound go back to Netherrealm through a portal on the sand. As Vera leaves the beach, Spike pops back out of their portal, flipping her off behind her back, angering her. Vera and her group then leave the beach for the foreshore, surrounded by police (plus one clown). Vera tells the rest of the group to get ready to engage in a lot of sexual bribery to get out of this predicament.
