The episode opens with

teaching an elementary school class, talking about how one can lead a good life but still be sent to

As class begins, she and her students sing about learning and the day to come when a student sitting in a corner reminds her today is her husband's birthday. Mrs. Mayberry panics, as she forgot and hadn't gotten him a present when one of her students asks if they can call him as a happy birthday surprise, which Mrs. Mayberry agrees to, using the classroom computer to video call her husband, Jarold. However, much to her and the class' shock and horror, Jarold Mayberry is in bed having sex with another woman, which causes Mrs. Mayberry to snap and storm out of the classroom, throwing a student who tries to calm her down through the ceiling on her way out. Driving home, her students watch the video call in horror as Mrs. Mayberry violently kills her husband and attacks the woman with a gun and chainsaw before she reminds her students to work on their times tables and takes her own life upon realizing what she has done in front of them.

It then cuts to

I.M.P Headquarters

, where Mrs. Mayberry, now a Fallen, has been telling him the story as background for why she wants to hire the company's services. Spike mocks her, telling her that I.M.P only kills targets in the living world, and everyone she wants I.M.P to kill is already dead. Mrs. Mayberry then reveals that while she successfully killed Jarold, the woman he had been in bed with, Martha, not only survived but is also treated as a hero because of it, appearing on talk shows, receiving two million dollars in donations and complimented by everyone around her, infuriating Mayberry.

In the office lobby,

When Mill tells him to stop shaking or he'll hit Luna, Mox tells her he can't bring himself to shoot the picture, as he doesn't want to kill an entire innocent family. Mill reminds him that if the client wants, he must do it. At the same time, Luna berates him, saying appearances can be deceiving and invents reasons why the family in the picture could be horrible people, which Mill agrees with. Mill then tells him morality isn't their business; killing is. Just as Mox is steeling himself to take the shot, Spike suddenly slams open the door to introduce Mrs. Mayberry to the rest of the staff, startling Mox, who fires the crossbow. The arrow ricochets around the room, bouncing off the walls and ceiling, hitting a computer monitor, the picture and the leg of a fish tank before Spike catches it in time to stop it from hitting Mrs. Mayberry in the eye. The fish tank then collapses, spilling water and the new electric eels within it across the floor before the eels set fire to the office, much to Spike's anger.

Outside the office, a fire crew packs up after extinguishing the blaze and Spike walks Mrs. Mayberry to a taxi, telling her they'll kill Martha within 24 hours or else it's free. Mox asks him when they made that offer and Spike angrily tells him it was when he had just set fire to the office in front of a client before asking if the

they used to access the living world survived the blaze. Luna confirms she grabbed the book to which Spike offers her a treat, which she declines, so Spike eats it himself, disgusting her. After Luna casts the spell needed to pass into the living world on the side of the building, Spike, Mill and Mox head through to kill their latest target.

Arriving at the lakeside home she is living in, Spike and Mox approach the house to find Martha, her husband Ralphie and their two children sitting down for dinner. Spike offers to let Mox kill the target, but upon seeing her family, he hesitates. Spike, telling Mox he took too long to decide, goes to shoot Martha himself with a sniper rifle, but Mox asks him if they're killing a family. Spike tells him they're not killing a family; they're ruining a family by killing the mother. Mox makes him think about what they're doing before he shoves the rifle, causing Spike to miss. Inside the house, having heard the gunshot, Martha asks Ralphie what it was and Ralphie responds, saying whatever it was is going to be turned into tomorrow night's dinner while bringing out his shotgun from under the table and taking a more sinister appearance. Martha, who then drinks a whole glass of wine, grabs her own shotgun and becomes insidious herself, orders her children to bring out their own guns, which they do.

Outside, Spike furiously chastises Mox for ruining his shot. Mox apologizes as the family seemed so innocent and caused him to panic. Spike then argues that no one is truly innocent and that all humans are evil from birth. As they bicker, a gunshot suddenly blows a hole in the wall and wounds Spike's arm. Spike commands everyone to scatter and retreat, which he and Mill do while Mox hides in the bushes as another gunshot blows out the window through which Martha and Ralphie pursue his co-workers. Mox then attempts to run away but is captured by the tail. Ralphie chases after Mill, who runs to the jetty and hides in the water of the lake. Ralphie looks for her holding a bottle as a weapon, then Mill ambushes him by jumping through the jetty itself. After a brief fight, he manages to hit her out with the bottle, knocking her unconscious.

Meanwhile, Mox wakes up tied to a chair to the sight of the kids. He attempts to make peace with them, but then they creepily tell him "It's nice to have a new critter to play with", disturbing him. Mox then looks around the room, seeing several taxidermized human heads and pieces of tanned human skin mounted on the walls and a roast human on the table in front of him, revealing to his horror how these people are cannibals.

In the forest, Spike continues to run as Martha shoots at him and cackles. As she goads Spike, he manages to find refuge behind a tree but as soon as he hides, he gets a call from

. After fumbling with the phone a bit, he answers the call and informs Stellar of how this is a terrible time, to which Stellar questions if there is ever NOT a wrong time. Spike then demands what he wants, and Stellar answers, saying it's about the grimoire which Spike borrowed from him and he needs to perform his own duties. Just then, Martha blasts a hole in the tree Spike was hiding behind after hearing Spike talking on the phone, forcing him on the run again. Stellar then offers to let Spike continue using the book in exchange for "favors" and Spike demands him to keep the language of the deal simple, so Stellar does so: Once a month on the full moon, Spike will return the book to him, as well as have sex with him like before and in exchange, Stellar will let Spike keep the book for the rest of the time. Wanting to end the call quickly, Spike agrees to this bargain. An elated Stellar goes off on an expletive-laden rant about his sexual fantasies with Spike, which distracts him long enough for Martha to knock the phone out of his hand and restrain him at the neck with the butt of her shotgun. Though interested in seeing an actual Netherspawn, Martha isn't going to let him or his fellow Netherspawns harm her family. She vows to send them back to Netherrealm while Stellar continues his fantasy rant over the phone.

Back in the house, Mox sees a fire through the windows. Realizing Mill and Spike are in trouble, he rocks the chair backward and onto the daughter, freeing himself with the knife she had and runs towards the fire. There, Martha and Ralphie have Spike and Mill tied to a stake and are preparing to burn them alive to return them to Netherrealm. As Martha ignites the wood and expects them to incinerate, Spike and Mill just sit there, telling her Earth flames can't harm hellions like the two of them as Spike mocks their efforts and offers to fake it to make them feel better. After her brief disappointment, Martha then decides to simply shoot Spike in the mouth but before she can, she is shot in the head by Mox, who retrieved the sniper rifle he and Spike had lost back at the house.

As Ralphie runs back to the house in a panic, Mox frees the pair from the still burning stake. While Mill is happy to see Mox and hugs him, Spike tells him he's not getting paid for the job before collapsing to the ground clutching his gunshot wound. Mox apologizes to his boss for interfering with the assassination as he helps Spike to his feet. Spike accepts the apology and pulls him into a hug but strictly warns him if the imp pulls a stunt such as that ever again, he'll "fuck" both him and Mill. Spike calls Luna to open a portal back to Netherrealm, but Mox tells him he left some things at the house.

Heading through the woods, Mox passes Spike's phone, where Stellar is still raving about what he wants to do in bed with Spike and reaches the house. There, he confronts the family and tells them how while they're monsters, he's not going to kill them and instead is going to call 911. After mistakenly trying to call them with a universal remote Ralphie got for his kids, Mox calls them with the kitchen phone before leaving the house.

Rejoining the others at the portal on a hill overlooking the house, Mox pauses as the others return to Netherrealm. At the same time, he watches the police arrive at the house. Instead of the husband and his kids being apprehended, the house is obliterated with the family still inside by a missile fired from a police helicopter. Mox looks at the demolished house in shock before Spike grabs him by the neck and drags him through the portal. Back in Netherrealm, Mrs. Mayberry and the other I.M.P staff congratulate him for killing Martha at a party, celebrating the job with a cake, ignoring the distressed expression on his face as the episode ends.
