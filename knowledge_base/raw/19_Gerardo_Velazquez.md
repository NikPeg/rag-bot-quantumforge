Gerardo was a middle-aged tanned-skin man of Mexican descent with a hefty build. He was balding with short gray hair on the sides of his head, and had thick eyebrows and mustache with a goatee and stubble around his chin. He also featured a single gold tooth in his mouth.

He wore a large, red-and-black-striped sombrero with golden poms, a beige button-up shirt under a black vest with a gold skull and red crossbones symbols, brown pants held by a black belt with golden skull buckles with crossed eyes, and black boots.

From what is shown of Gerardo, he was a passionate farmer who took great pride in his goats. He came across as self-centered, as he made

, whom he mistook for the Chupacabra, an attraction for the townsfolk to parade his capture and swindle them out of their money, showing a greedy side. He even doesn't care much for authority, as he insists that Agents One and Two should pay him to see the "Chupacabra" despite the two being with the government.

He was also observant and cunning, as seen when he acted oblivious of Blitzo's attempts to talk to him, as well as taking advantage of Blitzo's time unconscious to strip him of the tools to help him escape.

Despite his egotism, Gerardo genuinely loved his goats, resorting to shooting
