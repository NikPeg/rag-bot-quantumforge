Charlie returns to the hotel enraged at what Lucifer had done and confronts him about it. Lucifer fesses up and says Vaggie was the one who told him to do so. This causes a rift between the two causing Vaggie to snap and tell Charlie she is playing into Vox's game which is making everything worse. However, Charlie angirly refuses to listen and turns her back on Vaggie, losing trust in her and tells her off, claiming if she can't stand by her side she can fix everything on her own. Having had enough, Vaggie snaps back and leaves in a huff. Lucifer attempts to console his daughter but she coldly and selfishly pushes him away. Saddened by being separated from his daughter again, Lucifer sadly teleports away.

The episode ends off with Vaggie, venting her frustrations drinking at the bar, Lucifer sadly packing all his stuff preparing to leave the hotel and lastly Charlie who again attempts to call her mom but can't get her on her phone with her sadly curling up in a ball.

By the end of the episode Charlie is on bad terms with both Vaggie and Lucifer and feels more alone as even trying to call Lilith only to be met with a voice recording brings her more despair.

Upon Vaggie visiting Lucifer's room, an assortment of presents can be seen on his bed. These are evidently from the other sins, given to Lucifer in an attempt to entice him to return to the group amid his absence.

While several are gift-wrapped and unable to be identified, seven of them are on full display, representing the other six sins:

An oversized cowboy hat presumably from Satan.

A bag of candies from Beelzebub.

Multiple large stacks of cash with a note reading "This is a bribe come back -Mammon" from... Mammon.

An apple-shaped ball gag, presumably from Asmodeus.

A vial of perfume and a stylish travel bag with a deep sea motif, both presumably from Leviathan.

And a sleep aid plushie of Belphegor, presumably from Belphegor herself.

The songs "VOX POPULI" and "VOX DEI" are Latin phrases, translating to "the voice of the people" and "the voice of God" respectively, which together complete the phrase, "Vox populi, vox Dei", meaning "the voice of the people is the voice of God", which is fitting because Vox has become the voice of the people and plans to using this voice to become a god.

During VOX DEI, when Vox sings out the line he is the Omega, the Symbol of Omega appears on his screen for a brief moment.
