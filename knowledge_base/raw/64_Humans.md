Humans are very susceptible to dying in various ways, including natural causes such as old age or disease, and any large amount of damage to their bodies such as being shot, stabbed, dismembered, asphyxiated, or receiving hard enough blunt force trauma. In addition, large doses of drugs or alcohol can cause death in humans.

Demons have mixed views on humanity. For example, Millie says that humans are full of "secret nasties" and it often results to many human souls entering Hell. Angels are willing to interact with living humans, such as the Cherubs who are sent to save humans from death and/or reform them into virtuous people, allowing them a higher chance to enter Heaven as angels.
