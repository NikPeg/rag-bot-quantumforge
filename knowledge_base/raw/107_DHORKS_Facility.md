The facility is a non-descript urban structure located within an unknown city. The exterior of the building is protected by an array of surveillance cameras, searchlights, barbed wire fences, and armed guards, albeit equipped with melee weaponry.

The interior of the facility is similarly nondescript, consisting of mostly long, grey, and brown corridors. Like the outside, it possesses an extensive surveillance camera network.

The facility is inhabited by a large contingent of D.H.O.R.K.S personnel, the majority of whom appear to be working as guards. They are similarly equipped with only melee weapons for self-defense, with the exception of some utilising shurikens/throwing stars. The extensive security system allows it to quickly detect any intruders and raise an alarm. If an alarm is raised, large segments of the facility can be quickly locked down with large blast doors, and red-alarm lights can be activated to impair visibility.

Deep within the facility, it houses an interrogation room capable of holding multiple subjects for interrogation. The rooms are capable of dispensing gaseous substances, such as 'truth gas', to assist in the interrogation of subjects.
