The episode opens on a concert in the

hosted by the Sin of Greed - the unsympathetic and egotistical Mammon. The venue is sold out and among the crowd are a teenage

. When Mammon bombastically unveils himself, he announces a clown pageant, the winner of which will become the new face of his clown-themed brand (which he not-so-secretly just wants to use as an exploitative sales gimmick). In the crowd, Fizzarolli is loving everything about it while Blitzo remains skeptical. Mammon then lets his clowns loose on the audience.

When the event ends and Blitzo and Fizzarolli are walking home, Fizzarolli tells Blitzo that he'd like nothing more than to win that pageant and work with Mammon. However, Blitzo states it's pointless considering the fame and fortune they've already netted from

their circus

and it's not worth becoming Mammon's creepy mascot. Fizzarolli elaborates that it's not about the fame and fortune, but rather the chance to work alongside his lifelong idol and goes as far as to label him as an inspiration. Simply, Blitzo tells him Mammon is definitely something, hiding the truth of how he doesn't like him and goes on to ask if all the crazy antics that come with Mammon's endorsement are worth their life savings. When Fizzarolli says it's totally worth it, a stranger is lurking in the shadows. He asks Blitzo if he could actually do it with hard work and dedication and Blitzo tells him he's the only hellion who would ever be the right fit for all things "clown", if anyone. Just then, the two of them are intercepted by

Arick Burnz

, an obsessive fan of their circus who creeps out Fizzarolli to no end and tries pitching his own idea for an act in their performance involving a romantic Ballroom Dance. Hostilities escalate between him and Blitzo, who recognizes him as a creepy stalker he regularly sees snooping around their dressing rooms, and the two of them leave in huff. Arick melodramatically and arrogantly denounces them out of spite for turning their back on him, mostly due to his extremely uncomfortable demeanor. Upon hearing his harsh words, Fizzarolli questions if his acts are just trash and Blitzo tells him to not dwell on the opinions of one obsessive asshole. Despite this, Fizzarolli still feels vulnerable, claiming that if he wants to succeed, he needs to be perfect.

After having won a clown pageant, Fizzarolli's obsessive training and vigorous preparation has paid off and he becomes the face of Mammon's clown brand, and is part of an advertisement to sell plush toys and robotic simulacrums in his image for $69.99.

In present day at

Asmodeus' Tower

, Fizzarolli is scrambling to put on his foundation that conceals an injury on his face in preparation for his 10th Clown Pageant when

walks in. However, he doesn't understand what Fizzarolli's worries are all about, claiming that Mammon's clown pageant is stupid. But all Fizzarolli can say is he wants to be perfect for Mammon and to make him proud. As a result, Asmodeus tells him there's no such thing as perfect and he tries to convince him maybe it's time for him to retire from the pageant. Better yet, he suggests taking a vacation someplace where he doesn't have to fend off creepy fans. However, stating that he's always had to fend off creepy fans even before the simulacrums, Fizzarolli claims this is just something that he has to do. Asmodeus explains that he doesn't like the idea of Fizzarolli having to fend off fans, much less designing sex toys in Fizzarolli's likeness for Mammon. However, Fizzarolli sticks with his earlier statement that working for Mammon is a big deal to him, especially since he's been his idol since he was five years old, claiming that he can't let him down by not competing. Asmodeus tells Fizzarolli Mammon is not worth working for, claiming that he's known Mammon since the beginning of

existence and he's always sucked and as much as he loves Fizzarolli, he doesn't want him doing the clown schtick for someone's approval. Fizzarolli acknowledges Asmodeus and he tells him that this pageant is for him and he just doesn't want to lose.

, Blitzo is watching TV and eating cereal in his apartment when he gets a call from Asmodeus. The two exchange hellos and Asmodeus explains Fizzarolli's obsession with the annual clown pageant, hoping Blitzo could help him in explaining to him that it's a waste of time. But Blitzo isn't worried since Fizzarolli is undefeated until Asmodeus tells him that Fizzarolli doesn't listen when he tries telling him that Mammon is a selfish, manipulative piece of shit. What he's hoping for is, because of their history and newly rekindled friendship, Blitzo could talk some sense into him. This makes Blitzo agree to come to the Clown Pageant.

The trio arrive via limousine at Mammon's theater and Fizzarolli asks why Blitzo is accompanying them. Asmodeus tells him because the secrecy of their romance doesn't allow the two of them to be seen together, Blitzo has to accompany him for security detail, citing how he was able to keep him safe when were kidnapped by

. Asmodeus departs and Fizzarolli is greeted by Mammon, who tells of his stiff competition. When Blitzo sees Mammon ridiculing Fizzarolli over "minor details" which could prove detrimental to his sales image, Blitzo doesn't hesitate to denounce one of Mammon's own performances. He tells Fizzarolli to keep smiling and he departs, leaving Blitzo to not hesitate to say he does not like him at all. Fizzarolli tells Blitzo while he still doesn't know the real reason Asmodeus brought him to the venue, he orders him not to talk back to his boss. When Blitzo questions why he needs this gig, pointing out his apparent affair with the Asmodeus, Fizzarolli says he just needs it and proceeds with smiling and waving to his fans on the red carpet. However, tensions arise when his competition rolls in:

Glitz and Glam

, twin sisters with sharp claws and sharper tongues. Fizzarolli tries to exchange pleasantries with them, but they just denounce him as a fugly imp and are looking forward to crushing anything which dares interfere with their victory. This makes Blitzo encourage Fizzarolli to bring his A-Game for the sole purpose of spiting them.

Fizzarolli and the twins compete in the opening act while

intense music

plays and they tie for 666 on the score board. Afterwards, Mammon hosts the admission for meet-&-greets with the stars and refuses to allow Fizzarolli to skip it when he doesn't feel comfortable with meeting the fans at this time. Fizzarolli tries to stand up for himself, but Mammon selfishly pressures him into doing it for the sake of the show. Blitzo tries to get him to explain why he lets Mammon walk all over him, but Fizzarolli still refuses to open up to him. He greets the fans and marches past his discomfort. When he thanks them all for showing up, he is greeted by a young imp boy who tells him in sign language how he's a big fan. Fizzarolli wholesomely greets him on his level and tells everyone to be excited for the finale. Unfortunately, that's when Arick returns and denounces his career in front of everybody, still embittered by being turned away all those years ago. Fizzarolli has his back against the wall when he menacingly approaches him and Blitzo swoops in, ready to blow him to kingdom come if he doesn't leave at once but just before he can, Fizzarolli is overtaken by anxiety and Blitzo settles for hitting him away. Arick leaves and swears revenge, Mammon appears, inquiring on what just happened. Fizzarolli feels pressured into telling him everything's fine and Mammon tells him in light of whatever this is, the sisters will go on first to give Fizzarolli time to prepare. Blitzo questions him as to why he's really doing the pageant again, repeating Asmodeus' earlier claim of clown pageant being stupid. All Fizzarolli tells him is this job is all that matters and he'll lose everything otherwise. Blitzo denounces Mammon as a trash fire using Fizzarolli for everything, but Fizzarolli defends him and he states that it's all part of the process to make him perfect, leaving Blitzo questioning as to what he'd need to be perfect for.

The twins go on and their

performance

knocks the audience dead. Watching them stresses Fizzarolli to the point of making him flee for his dressing room. When he looks in the mirror, he sees that he's sweating so much, his foundation is running and exposing his scar. Seeing the amount of emotional trouble Fizzarolli is in, Asmodeus risks exposure to appear in his dressing room to check on him. Fizzarolli insists he's fine when Asmodeus can clearly see he's not. He tries getting through to Fizzarolli once again, but he is still stubborn and he claims he needs to prove himself by winning. That's when Asmodeus explains Fizzarolli only acts like he needs to be this perfect clown because Mammon is perpetually forcing this image onto him. Then, Fizzarolli confesses he has his perfect life and Asmodeus because of Mammon and needs to win to prove he's still worthy. As a result of this, Asmodeus asks how Fizzarolli could possibly lose him and Fizzarolli tearfully explains Asmodeus is only Fizzarolli's lover for what he is at his best. Fizzarolli finally reaches his breaking point when he yanks off his hat and shows Asmodeus what's left of his horns and his scar-ridden face, denouncing himself as a barely adequate partner or mascot for any king of sin when

is who he really is. He believes he owes everything to him because without Mammon, he's nothing. Finally knowing the truth, Asmodeus explains to the vulnerable Fizzarolli Mammon didn't do anything to make Fizzarolli the great success in life. He did because he was always the inspiration Asmodeus fell in love with, and he doesn't hesitate to tell him he is loved. Asmodeus finishes his heartfelt statement by telling Fizzarolli he's a way better performer than Mammon ever was - not something Fizzarolli could trust in himself, until now. Afterwards, the two of them share a hug and Fizzarolli is finally ready to rock the show, knowing that his Asmodeus will still be waiting for him, whether he wins it or not. After a

heartfelt duet

, they share a kiss, until they are interrupted by Arick, who returns with a dagger. Thankfully, Blitzo is there to blow his head off and he sees that the two of them really are an item and then he sarcastically congratulates them on being "fucking hypocrites".

With the audience losing hope in seeing Fizzarolli's performance, Mammon addresses them when the stage lights up and he takes his seat with his Fizzarolli simulacrum harem waiting for him. Fizzarolli performs a heartfelt

telling the story of the abuse he's dealt with all his life and how he's ready to end this chapter with a bang. His performance goes off like fireworks and when it's all over, he finally quits in front of everybody and stands up to Mammon. He tries to walk out on him, before he transforms into his true form. Seeing Fizzarolli in danger, Asmodeus transforms too and he prepares for battle. Mammon tries to blackmail Asmodeus into standing down by threatening to reveal his romance with Fizzarolli, but instead Asmodeus announces it to the entire audience himself. However, instead of laughing and mocking them, the fans instead fangirl and they already knew they were in love and publicated it everywhere, much to Mammon's shock and confusion. Finally rendered powerless, Mammon can do nothing except menacingly telling Asmodeus he's going to regret revealing his deepest darkest secret and disappears, releasing an explosion that brings the whole place down.

In the limousine ride home, Fizzarolli and Asmodeus are cuddling, when Blitzo asks the two of them "Who tops?". Fizzarolli groans in annoyance and Asmodeus smiles as the episode ends.

"Mammon's Theme" was a minor collaboration with Tom Cardy, the composer of "

This theme has two elements that are used as a "leitmotif" for Mammon: The melody itself (which is a play on the theme from the opera "
