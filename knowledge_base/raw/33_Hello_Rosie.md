As of this episode Vaggie has regrown her wings, and the residents of the hotel are now aware that she is an angel.

This also implies angels may have the ability to regenerate their wings.

Before the episode aired, Amazon Prime had accidentally released a screenshot of Vaggie with her wings as part of a series of sneak peek material, which was then immediately removed.

Carmilla Carmine reveals that she's already aware that Vaggie was an exorcist based on the latter's x eyepatch and her angelic spear.

Additionally, she also reveals that angels can also be killed by their own weapons.

Carmilla is revealed to have angelic steel ballet shoes, of which she reveals she used in order to successfully kill an Exorcist.

Susan, as well as the "Shrunken Head Keychain", are listed as among the official trading cards that can be collected in the packs sold by Amazon.

According to Vivziepop, the entire concept of Susan was a after-the-fact alteration "for reasons". She added that writing Alastor beefing with a random old lady was fun too.

Rosie teasing about how Charlie was Alastor's girlfriend before saying he's "an ace in the hole" is a reference to how despite Alastor being aromantic asexual, he is not aware of the modern labels.

When Rosie first grabs him, Alastor bleats like a fawn, which is a sign of happiness and contentment for deer.

Charlie and Alastor finally make a deal, in which Charlie got information about the exorcists in exchange for a favor for Alastor at a time of his choosing that harms no one.

Charlie revealed when she was venting to Alastor that she and Vaggie have been together for three years.

This is the first time Charlie is shown to have goat-like hooves for feet.

It is revealed that Rosie is the leader of the people of Cannibal Town, and that the citizens will only do something if everyone agree to it.

Background designer Ashleigh Beevers revealed that the bass surrounded by a fence in Rosie's room is based off how during production Ashleigh had to put a cage around her real-life bass because of her dog, to which Sam Miller found it funny and requested for a fence to be put in the show.
