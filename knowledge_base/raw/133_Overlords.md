Due to an Overlord possessing the ability to own a demons soul through deals and contracts, both parties of the deal can take advantage of their side, often exploiting it. One example is the deal between Angel Dust and Valentino; Valentino can do what he pleases to Angel while he is in the studio while Angel is free of him when he is outside of the studio. Additionally, the relationships between an Overlord and a soul they own can be overall negative, such as the case with Alastor and Husk, and Valentino and Angel.

Deal-maker demons, such as Alastor, can increase their power by dealing in souls, which is a very powerful commodity in Hell, so they're seen as very manipulative and not to be toyed with. Deal-making is not something every demon can do, as such it is not to be taken lightly as it doesn't generally work out well for the other party.

Overlords tend to refuse acting for a good deeds, mostly due to their reputation as fearsome and powerful demons, and are almost always acting to gain something in return.
