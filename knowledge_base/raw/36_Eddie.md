Eddie is a child who has a strong lisp when he speaks. He is short and slightly chubby with shaggy light brown hair and freckles. He also is missing an upper tooth.

He wears a blue baseball cap, an orange shirt with an image of a ringed planet, black pants, and black shoes.

Eddie is accidentally shot by Moxxie during a hit, and then rushed to a hospital where the doctors use defibrillators on him to save his life. The group are unceremoniously thrown out of the hospital upon learning that the imps don't have any insurance, nor even know what insurance actually is. Eddie pretends to be paralyzed as the imps take him to Hell, where they begin to argue amongst themselves until he drops the paralysis act and chastises them in a manner that reveals he is a jerk and angers everyone, calling Blitzo a selfish greedy clown, Moxxie a spineless jackass, and Millie a slut. However, he has nothing to say to Loona, telling her that he's a cat person.

After Loona reveals that she just got word from their client that Eddie is the target I.M.P was hired to kill all along, Eddie is killed by Blitzo. After Blitzo shoots him, the imps proceed to dismember his corpse before putting his remains in a bag and then dropping it into the arms of his mother on Earth during a news broadcast.

Eddie appears in a cameo as part of a group of witnesses that Millie and I.M.P slaughter in her dream scenario of how she wants to handle I.M.P's first hit and Moxxie's problem of witnesses. Eddie is torn in half in a gory fashion by Millie herself.

Eddie is mentioned on Blitzø's list of people he apologises to, being nicknamed "Annoying kid".
