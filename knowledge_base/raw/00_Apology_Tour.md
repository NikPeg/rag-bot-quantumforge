"Stolas' Garden" has some of its theme unused as an artistic choice that the production team made because the absence of music can draw viewers deeper into the characters' emotions and interactions. The cue bounces back and forth between Stolas' emotional theme and something more silly and aggressive for Blitzo.

"Montage" used snippets of different melodies from previous episodes, most of them originally composed by Jefferson Friedman.

"The Party" was made entirely with loops from LoopMasters. A dark orchestral bit with choir pops up for the cut-away gag with Stolas and the cue ends with some light orchestral backgrounds.
