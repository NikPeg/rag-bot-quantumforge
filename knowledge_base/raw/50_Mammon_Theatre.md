Mammon Theatre has large circus tents, like big tops, and several large boats sticking out behind them. The main tent has a long red carpet leading to the entrance, and a massive ship sticking out of the water behind it, with the front of the ship's hull having the appearance of being ripped apart with jagged spikes at the top and bottom.

Inside the massive ship is a gigantic concert stadium with a large stage at the center decorated with large curtains and the sigils of Mammon. There are multiple multicolored stage lights above the stage as well as several large boats messily jutting out behind the stage.
