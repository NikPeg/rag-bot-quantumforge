IMP MISSION: ORPHANS

The short begins with

arriving in the human world to kill their newest target: Mr.

Frank McTickly Wrigglers

, a children's entertainer. Blitzo wants to get this over with so he can have the rest of the day to spend with his daughter. Loona decides to get this over with and heads to Mr. Wrigglers' dressing room while Blitzo finds another way in.

When Loona finds Mr. Wrigglers, she's surprised that the door was open but Mr. Wrigglers says it's because he's eager to meet new friends, citing a banner celebrating orphans. Loona is caught off guard as Mr. Wrigglers seems keen on supporting her wish to kill him, although admitting that it's a little darker than he imagined. Blitzo appears, dressed as an orphan, wondering what is taking so long. Loona claims that she was about to kill him. Mr. Wrigglers asks for a last wish before he dies: to say goodbye to all of his children. Loona agrees, much to Blitzo's surprise.

After a montage of goodbyes to his children, Blitzo becomes frustrated when he finds out that they had wasted all day on Mr. Wrigglers' children. Mr. Wrigglers then requests that he look at the sunset as he dies, and Loona, warming up to the children's entertainer, agrees.

On the bench, Mr. Wrigglers thanks Loona for becoming his newest friend and, after hearing her doubts, urges her to show off her true self. But once Loona drops her disguise, he freaks out, causing Loona to accidentally shoot him, and then again when he throws sand at her in defense. Loona runs out of bullets before she can finish the job so Blitzo appears and smashes the man's head in with a brick repeatedly until it's turned into paste on the ground.
