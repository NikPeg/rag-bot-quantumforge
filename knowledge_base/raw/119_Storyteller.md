Sir Pentious asks Emily if this is good or bad, but she is unsure, and he then asks is Charlie should know, reminding her that she has to tell Charlie about his redemption and the lockdown. She leaves before Sir Pentious can ask to see his friends, much to his sadness, while Heaven is completely closed off as the force field completes itself.

Sir Pentious ends up sulking on his new bed, with the heavenly-made Egg Boiz surrounding him for comfort.
