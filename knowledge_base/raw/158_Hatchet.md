Hatchet is a slim white-skinned demon with a draconic snout full of sharp yellow teeth except missing one tooth, eyes with red sclera and yellow irises, and messy red hair with long horns protruding from the top of his head with two black stripes. He has black tattoos on his arms, black claws on his fingers, and black feet with two red toes. He also has a long mostly black, clubbed tail that has red spikes from the end.

Hatchet wears a black cropped tank top with rips at the hem, torn grey pants held up by a black belt with a red skull for a buckle, and a black collar with red spikes.

Hatchet’s pilot design is almost identical except for his horns being long tuffs of hair, more elaborate arm patterns, and whiter teeth.
