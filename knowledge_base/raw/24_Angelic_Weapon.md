These weapons are also powerful enough to harm, kill and even render demon nobles such as the Ars Goetia vulnerable. In the case of much more powerful demons and angels such as Lucifer, angelic weapons can be designed to be strong enough to subdue and even cause great discomfort, Lucifer calling it a "bad tickle", but aren't powerful enough for him to be killed by such weaponry, though Lucifer did become lightly injured when the Might of Lilith began malfunctioning.

The Exorcists themselves appear to hold little value to the weapons, and will readily discard them, often still left inside the last demon who was killed by it. As a result, large quantities of discarded angelic weapons are left abandoned following an Extermination. Demon scavengers will attempt to salvage any remaining angelic weapons following an Extermination to be sold on the black market, providing other demons a means of killing each other.
