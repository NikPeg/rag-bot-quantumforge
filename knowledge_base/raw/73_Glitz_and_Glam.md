Glitz and Glam are tall, slim and faded daphne blue-skinned humanoid demons with fish-like features - such as head fins similar to a that of a lionfish, and a pair of large, retractable fin-like wings on their back. They also have a pair of black-ended horns on top of their heads.

Both sisters sport eyes with cyan irises, deep teal sclera, slit pupils, large and extravagant eyelashes protruding from them, lighter-colored upper eyelids and black marks across them. They also have lengthy, extravagant and fin-like hair which are colored in multiple shades of teal and cyan, which are styled to resemble high ponytails with long bangs.

Their mouths have black lips with a black spot at each end, and sharp teeth inside.

Glitz's bangs are styled in a spiky, mohawk-like way,

colored in a bright teal with darker-colored edges and deep teal green stripes. Meanwhile, her ponytail is colored a bright teal with a darker-colored stripe running across it, along with three deep teal green spots and dark navy edges, as well as the interior colored in a shade of dark teal.

Her eyes have large bright teal fins with darker-colored edges, which acts as her eyelashes.

Glitz wears both a dark navy choker and a dark navy crop-top, with the top itself sporting a cyan pom-pom on the front and long sleeves; her left sleeve having both cyan trim and three cyan diamonds on it.

Her high-waisted miniskirt is seafoam green-and-dark teal in a triangle-like pattern, while her thigh-high tights are dark navy with a cyan pom-pom on each foot, as well as an asymmetrical-like pattern; the right having three cyan diamonds while the left has a seafoam green stripe. She accessorizes with fingerless gloves which are the same color as her top, choker and tights.

Glam's bangs are styled in a long, straight way,

colored in a bright teal with a slight darker-colored stripe, along with both three dark navy spots and a dark navy interior. Meanwhile, her ponytail is colored in a dark navy with bright teal edges and two cyan star-sparkles, as well as the interior colored in a shade of dark teal.

Her eyes have lengthy and spiky eyelashes which are entirely black, with small dots adorning each end.

Glam wears a high-collared deep teal crop-top that sports an upside-down heart-shaped cutout and long, dark navy sleeves; her right sleeve having three cyan circles while her left is detached from the top.

Around her waist is a seafoam green miniskirt which resembles a jester's collar with multiple triangular appendages and a ball on each end, worn over a set of dark navy tights which sported a cyan pom-pom on each foot, as well as an asymmetrical-like pattern; the right leg having a seafoam green stripe while the left leg has three cyan diamonds. She accessorizes with a set of bright teal gloves on her hands; the right over her sleeve while the left is under her sleeve.

Glitz and Glam are sassy, prideful and egotistic, as seen when they talk down to Fizzarolli in their

debut episode

. They believe themselves to be wholly superior to him and presumably the other contestants in the

Clown Pageant

They appeared to have a typical sibling relationship towards each other, not afraid to throw insults at each other while functioning perfectly as a team during their acts and also often seen holding hands and smiling at each other. This suggests that, in spite of not always getting along, the sisters still have a close bond.

There seems to be a noticeable difference in their personalities, with Glitz being the more energetic and excitable twin, as she is often seen laughing and smiling widely, and Glam is the more measured and stoic twin, as she is seen unamused or annoyed with a frown on her face. Additionally, Glitz seems more willing to use her sex appeal, having no problem with flashing the crowd during their song.
