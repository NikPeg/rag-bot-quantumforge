Charlie then reintroduces Sir Pentious to Alastor, who says he definitely remembers Sir Pentious now that he ruined his coat. Charlie gets Sir Pentious to apologize to Alastor, the former also returning the piece of fabric that he took off of Alastor’s coat. Alastor says that "not many people have managed to take even this much off" of him and it must have meant a lot to Sir Pentious, before burning it in front of him.

Later, Charlie, Vaggie, Angel and Sir Pentious are sitting in the parlor together, and Charlie is getting them to play a rhyming game as an icebreaker. Charlie and Sir Pentious each take turns, but Angel refuses, calling it stupid. Vaggie tells him that he better get used to it, because it was going to be his whole day. After this, Angel and Sir Pentious perform a skit while Charlie, Vaggie, Alastor, Husk and Niffty watch them. Angel Dust performs in complete deadpan, not liking the script, while Sir Pentious puts more effort into his acting. Charlie congratulates Sir Pentious, saying he would soon be redeemed if he kept going at the rate he was. Angel, looking sad, announces he's going to bed. Going to his room, he lies on his bed and listens to his voicemails from Valentino, which alternate between him being nice and him being threatening, until Valentino's voice echoes through the room, saying that Angel can't change and "addict trash" like him can't be redeemed.

Saddened by this, Angel goes downstairs to get a drink. While he's there, he notices Sir Pentious and follows him into another room, where he sees him putting a VoxTek camera on a shelf. Angel confronts him and accuses him of working for the Vees. When Sir Pentious denies this and calls him a "Whorebug", Angel attacks him, but Sir Pentious gets him off using hypnosis to stun him. Angel quickly shakes it off, though. When Charlie and Vaggie come into the room, Angel tells them that Sir Pentious was a spy for the Vees and shows them the camera as proof. Pentious, panicking, attempts to contact Vox through a watch that the Vees gave him. Vox laughs at him for getting caught so quickly, before saying that if the hotel's residents didn't kill him, he should kill himself.
