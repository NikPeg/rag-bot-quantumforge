IMP MISSION: ANTARCTICA

The episode begins with

arriving to Antarctica through a portal, having a target on an unknown "man-eating beast" who killed the client. Having been told that the beast is near the research station, they head inside and discover the dead bodies of the research crew.

Shortly after, they hear a roar and spot a very large leopard seal outside which Blitzo assumes is the culprit, before it charges towards them, seemingly with the intent to kill them. Blitzo's bullets fail to stop the seal, but Moxxie manages to blow its head off with his rifle. Believing they killed the target,

celebrates until Millie notices bite marks on the seal and hypothesizes that the seal was running from something else.

Moxxie then spots a bloodstained penguin on a high cliff and is ordered to kill it by Blitzo, only for a whole waddle of penguins to show up. Millie says the other penguins might be nice while they wonder who the imps are, until one calls them slurs (in penguin language) before giving chase. I.M.P barricade themselves inside the research station, with the huddle trying to get in, still saying slurs. Eager to flee, Blitzo tries to make the

Asmodean Crystal

on his wrist work but fails. Millie tells him because the crystal is from

, he must "caress" it, which means using his tongue, which Blitzo fails to do as well. Growing impatient, Moxxie decides to do it himself in a very drawn-out and awkward manner, which makes Blitzo and Millie blush, and the portal opens.

Once through the portal, Millie spots a gas canister. She lights a match and throws the matchbox back through, causing the research facility to explode, killing most of the penguins. The sole surviving penguin then curses out I.M.P as the episode ends.
