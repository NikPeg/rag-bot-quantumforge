The episode begins with Charlie, Vaggie, Husk, Cherri, Niffty, Emily, and Baxter formulating their plan to accept Vox's invitation to his party, split into groups of three to infiltrate his base, disable the cameras, rescue Angel, and finally disarm the

Might of Lilith

. Once inside, they intend to carry out their plan to stop the Vees, and Sir Pentious is tasked with inventing a broadcasting device that can act as a video conference between Heaven and Hell, to confirm that redemption is in fact possible.

Upon arriving to the party, Vox seizes the opportunity to antagonize Charlie about how her plans have continuously failed while Vaggie attempts to enlist the help of Carmilla, who refuses. The group splits into their respective operations, with Niffty accompanying Baxter. Before Vox is able to call on the help of a disgruntled Velvette and Valentino (who have slipped away from the event as Valentino has become annoyed with Vox's behavior), Baxter successfully overrides Vox's network and disables his voice, giving Charlie free reign to speak to the sinners.

Vaggie manages to infiltrate the inside of the angelic weapon, finding Lucifer being held captive. Although confused at first, she resolves that once she figures out how, she will free him.

Velvette manages to interfere in Baxter and Niffty's plans, with Niffty fighting viciously to make sure that Baxter stays safe, as that was the task entrusted to her by Vaggie. Because of her interference, though, Baxter is unable to connect to Heaven's network.

Husk and Cherri attempt to find Angel, which they succeed in, but they find him still in a dazed state. They are unable to break the mind control, and in an attempt to rescue him, Cherri attempts to abscond with him inside of a burlap sack. They are quickly discovered by Valentino, and they begin to fight.

The sinners are quickly agitated by Charlie's apparent false promise and begin to heckle her. Emily joins Charlie on stage and tells the crowd that Heaven believes in redemption, but Vox uses his hypnosis on the crowd to take back control, then captures Emily. Vox insults Charlie and then demands that she announce to the entirety of Hell that he is the strongest sinner and that no one else is as powerful.

Seeing the opportunity, Alastor

uses his one favor

on Charlie to make her comply with Vox's demand, thus breaking the terms of Alastor's deal with Rosie. Vox, while gloating and thanking Charlie "For sucking

much at everything", places his hands on her shoulders, which also frees the Alastor from his deal with Vox. Instantly breaking his bonds and gleefully deriding Vox as an "absolute idiot", Alastor advances on the stage, while Charlie rushes to free Emily. Though Vox tries to order Alastor back and objects that he did not

Charlie, Alastor reminds him that the terms of his bargain were to "not lay your

" on Charlie, and he gloats that Vox is "nothing if not predictable".

Enraged at having been tricked on a mere technicality, Vox declares his intent to simply beat Alastor into submission again with his newly-gained power. Alastor sarcastically says that Vox is the strongest Sinner in Hell, but that Vox's power seeking helped to sever Alastor's contract with Rosie. Now finally free and in complete control despite being injured and with his cane broken, Alastor remarks that he will enjoy Vox actually posing a challenge for him this time. Alastor and Vox both activate their full demon powers and engage in a fierce battle. Meanwhile, Niffty and Baxter fight Velvette.

Vox initially chases Alastor, trying to capture him, but the Radio Demon repeatedly avoids his attacks, despite being smashed into several skyscrapers. After an intense fight across the rooftops and freeways of Pentagram City, causing much destruction, Alastor manages to outmaneuver Vox at an overpass. Sending the Media Overlord flying by hurling a truck at him, Alastor launches himself after him at high speed, slamming at him and severing a large section of Vox's mechanical body. After a continued chase, he finally pins Vox, now lost most of mini screens and with a broken antenna, down on a rooftop and begins savagely ripping away his various cables out of his chest.

Unable to get Alastor off him, Vox calls for Shok.wav to take care of Alastor, which the demon shark does. Mocking the fact that Vox still can't take him on one-on-one, Alastor is forced to release Vox in order to evade Shok.wav. Vox, however, has little time to recuperate as he is accidentally sent flying by the shark's tail, landing back in the grounds of V Tower. As Alastor is chased across the skyscrapers by Shok.wav, an increasingly unhinged Vox tries to target him with the Might of Lilith. The cannon's beam narrowly misses repeatedly, destroying large sections of Pentagram City and killing many of its' inhabitants. Horrified, Charlie and Emily rush to help as many sinners to safety as they can.

During the ongoing battle with Valentino, Husk tells Cherri that Angel had once told him that Valentino has poor eyesight, and they could be able to exploit that weakness. Cherri then proceeds to beat up Valentino. Angel intervenes and begins to strangle Cherri, still under the influence of the mind control, then Husk punches him in the back of the head. Angel finally regains free will and realizes what he is doing, apologizing profusely. Valentino attempts to take Husk captive, but Husk easily outmaneuvers him with an exploding dice, sending Valentino out the window.

Velvette is still battling Niffty, and attempts to use holographic tombstones and knives (summoned with her phone), but is outdone by Baxter's hacking abilities. After having her hair burned off by an explosion, Velvette is blasted out the window by Niffty's flying kick. Baxter is about to start the broadcast, when an angelic beam from the Might of Lilith breaks through the window and destroys his laptop. Below, Velvette reassembles her broken body and frees the entangled Valentino. As they observe Vox firing the cannon wildly, Velvette declares that he is "losing his shit" while Valentino is getting into it, causing Velvette to reprimand him.

Carmilla Carmine finally joins Vaggie in disrupting the cannon's power; this also distracts Vox long enough to notice that the sinners have broken from his hypnosis and turned against him. They angrily denounce him as a traitor for having targeted Pentagram City. Enraged, Vox fires the weapon at Alastor for "taking everything from [him]". Emily intervenes to rescue Alastor, who has his arm snared in Shok.wav's jaws; however, one of her wings is shot off by The Might of Lilith. They both fall to the ground in front of V Tower, with Alastor gaining more wounds. Vox sees Alastor, Charlie, and Emily directly in front of the Might of Lilith's barrel. Seeing the opportunity to kill his rival, the Princess of Hell and a seraphim angel, he primes the weapon to fire. Carmilla warns him that firing the Might of Lilith again will overload. Vox, having lost all reason, disregards her warning and prepares to fire the cannon in a kamikaze maneuver, declaring his willingness to do whatever takes to "wipe the smile off Alastor's face", and saying he doesn't care what happens, he sheds a single tear.

Just as Vox is about to fire, Velvette kicks him off the cannon, with both her and Valentino declaring that it is over. Vox angrily dismisses them, declaring himself a god, but Valentino rips off Vox's TV head from his body, defeating him but not killing him. Valentino gathers his fellow Vees and tries to fly away, but fails as his wings have been damaged by Husk's dice. Unfortunately, the Might of Lilith is already overloading, putting half the city in danger of destruction. Emily attempts to stop the weapon, with Charlie quickly joining her, followed by Husk and Niffty,

combining their power to contain the weapon

; Vaggie, Angel, Cherri, and Baxter join hands with them.

Suddenly Lute and Abel arrive, to retrieve Emily on Sera's orders, but Lute goes after Vaggie for revenge. Charlie protests that removing Emily's power will cause the Might of Lilith to explode, but Lute is perfectly willing to let this happen. She successfully goads Vaggie into a rematch in hopes of killing her to hurt Charlie. Abel initially has a panic attack, but finally stands up for himself, asserts his authority as the new leader of the Exorcists, and forces Lute to stand down before she can kill Vaggie, ordering her back to Heaven.

Emily declares her power is not enough, though Charlie tearfully declares that it is okay. Seeing that both Charlie and an Angel are willing to sacrifice their lives in an effort to save the sinners, Carmilla and Zestial are moved to help them, adding their own power. Joined by Maestro, Zeezi, Prick, and Hatchet, the group begins to encase the cannon in a sphere of energy, though it still isn't quite enough.

From the sidelines, a watching Alastor is joined by Rosie, who berates him for not helping them. Alastor demands compensation in exchange for his aid; when Rosie declares she won't reward a "snake", Alastor taunts her that everyone will die unless she does. Unfazed by Rosie's remark that he really is a demon, Alastor says that she knew his "game" since their first meeting, and holds out his broken staff, indicating the price for his assistance.

A moment later, Rosie and Alastor, the latter with his staff repaired, join Charlie and the group of Overlords, allowing them to completely encase the cannon. Finally, even Velvette and Valentino join them to save their own lives. Vox, left as a disembodied head, protests hysterically but can do nothing. The combined strength of everyone finally lifts the Might of Lilith into the sky, where it is vaporized in a burst of light, saving Pentagram City.

After the weapon is destroyed, the Overlords agree to never mention the incident and having aided Charlie. Sir Pentious is able to get his signal to Hell and provide living proof that redemption is possible, validating Charlie's claims. Sera expresses deep remorse for starting the exterminations of Hell; Sir Pentious is also finally able to have a brief conversation with Cherri. Angel feels so guilty about having been a spy for Vox and nearly strangling Cherri that he refuses to return to the Hotel in an attempt to protect his friends

With the war finally over, things return to a new normal, with the Hotel's reputation restored and the numerous sinners coming, who are staying there actually wanting to redeem themselves. Alastor, having finally broken his deal with Rosie, regains his full powers and decides to stay at the Hotel for reasons unknown, though grimly eyeing Lucifer. Vox has lost everyone's approval and Valentino has becoming the new boss of VoxTek, and portraying him as the hero who saved the day. Heaven has fully come around to accepting sinners into Heaven once they redeem themselves, with the exception of Lute. Charlie and Vaggie recieve their new badges, with the former's saying she's the head counselor, while Vaggie's has her new name as "Vaggi, and is the Hotel's manager. Charlie calls her mom about the news, glad to have everything turning out better than ever, and expresses a wish that her mom could have seen everything that happened, and ends her message, which is when Lilith finally calls her back, much to Charlie's shock.
