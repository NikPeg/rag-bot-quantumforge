At a mansion window on Earth, Moxxie watches as Luigi scolds his son Paulie over killing one of his most trusted confidantes, who Paulie claims was a rat, but Luigi tells him off for not caring about the family matters. Moxxie enters the building to complete his mission, but is forced to hide behind the statue of an angel when he heard Luigi coming. Paulie starts praying to the angel when Moxxie accidentally swallows a spider and sneezes, making Paulie think the angel is talking to him and has to grant an "angel wish", which Moxxie goes along with to fool him. Moxxie finds that he and his target have something in common, namely how they both have a mafia boss father who doesn't care for them, and has them under their grasp like a tool whom they make do their bidding, which makes him change his mind about completing the mission.

He convinces Paulie to talk to the members of another mob, and get them to fake his death so he can leave his father's grasp. However, he was unable to convince them and was going to be killed, had Moxxie not saved him by killing the mobsters, without revealing himself.

Moxxie then asks Paulie about what went wrong and Paulie tells him that he told the mobsters about him knowing an angel and they assumed that he was crazy. While Moxxie suggests to Paulie that he should get a passport out of the country, Luigi comes in and tells Paulie that the Mortadella mob wants Paulie's head, and explains he is going to give his own life so Paulie stays safe and becomes the mafia's new leader, and tells him that he has been hard on Paulie to prepare him for this occasion and tells him that he loves him and that Family is everything while telling him to take care of his mother. Moxxie questions if that is how a father and son relationship should have been, while Paulie is ecstatic that his father is finally out of the picture and that he gets to be the new leader, however, Moxxie told him that the deal was to get him away from a life of crime, but Paulie tells him that they didn't have that sort of deal and thought that he was just laying low while the angel killed his father. Moxxie doesn't like Paulie's devious decision, and in disgust and anger due to Paulie's callousness, he crushes Paulie with the angel statue, killing him. Then, in disguise, he reveals to Luigi that his son is dead and walks away.

Paulie Paesano, the intended target, is shown as a mirror parallel of Moxxie:

Despite both being sons of mob bosses, Moxxie left the mafia and that life behind while Paulie craved to take his father's place.

Crimson was abusive towards Moxxie, which caused Moxxie to cut ties with him while Luigi really loved Paulie and was willing to sacrifice himself for his son, with Paulie being happy that he would die so he could take over.

"New Joisey" is likely a misspelling of "New Jersey" due to the client's Italian-American pronunciation.

It is shown that in Hell there is a leech-catching activity equivalent to fishing on Earth called "leeching", and there are competitions demons can enter to try catch big leeches.

"Paesano" is Italian for "countryman" or "fellow townsman" and a common Italian name.

This short shows Moxxie upset over his treatment from his father that was shown in "

Exes and Oohs

", and his desire to help Paulie leave the mafia like he did.
