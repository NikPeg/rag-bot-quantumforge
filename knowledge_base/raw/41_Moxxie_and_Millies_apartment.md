The walls of the entire apartment have a musical note-themed wallpaper. The entryway door is brown with a peephole, a small carpet, and two daggers stabbed into the corner of the doorframe. Beside the door is Moxxie and Millie's wedding photo above a small drawer table with eye flowers in a green vase and two candles. The living room has a couch with rips and several daggers in it, a low coffee table on a carpet, and a small flat screen television. The living room wall has three playbills, three daggers, and a pipe running up to the roof.

Moxxie and Millie's bedroom walls have several musical-related items. The bed has a large bedhead with jewels and a heart-like design on the front, and several daggers stabbed in it. On Moxxie's side of the bed is a bedside table with an alarm clock. On Millie's side of the bed is what appears to be a crooked lamp, and a dagger stabbed into a bedside table.
