KeeKee is a small demon-cat with a singular eye sporting a red iris and yellow sclera, and has multiple eyes and a mouth hidden in her tail.

She has a red outline and scruffy, mostly black colored fur with white patches, a white heart-shaped face, half of a white heart-shaped mark on both of her ears, white front paws, and white at the tip of her tail. She wears a red collar around her neck with a circular gold tag hanging off of the front.

Similar to a lot of cats, KeeKee is quiet and usually distant, but on occasions seeks affection from people around her, such as

Sir Pentious

. As stereotypical of a cat, she enjoys napping, sitting on peoples' laps, and getting pets. She is also curious and observant, as she watched and levitated to get a better view of Charlie singing
