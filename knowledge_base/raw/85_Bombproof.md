Bombproof is a dark gray horse with black stripes on his body and a flaming mane and tail. He has two pairs of orange glowing eyes, a set of glowing fangs, and what appears to be orange horseshoes. His lower body possesses a lava-like appearance.

Enhanced Speed

- As a horse, Bombproof shows to be capable of running fast, though it is unknown on how fast he can actually run.

Flaming Body

- It possesses a lava-like underbelly and a flaming mane and tail, hinting it has some form of pyrokinesis, though this has yet to be confirmed.
