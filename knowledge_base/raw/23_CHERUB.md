The Cherubs tell Lyle, convinced he's being haunted by ghost orphans, that all the people his technology saved and went to Heaven sent them to protect him. Blitzo crashes through the window as Moxxie and Millie use the door and attempt to convince him to go through with the suicide, asking him what he can do with his money now he's old.

The Cherubs ask if that's a serious question, as he can commit his fortune to philanthropy, which Lyle disagrees with, asking the Cherubs why they won't let him die. Blitzo tells Lyle they can help, asking Moxxie what they brought. Millie disagrees, saying it smells like he hasn't been out of bed in months before leaning in for a better smell, which causes her to vomit. The Cherubs tell him life can be beautiful at any age and that they'll show him, to the disappointment of Lyle and I.M.P.

The Cherubs take Lyle to a hill overlooking a forest and tell him that if he commits suicide, he'll miss out on all of nature's beauty. Blitzo, who, like the rest of I.M.P, is now in an animal costume, insults the Cherubs and tells Lyle that nature isn't that beautiful, showing him a group of rabbits and squirrels being attacked by wolves through a pair of binoculars a bear then attacks the wolves, but the bear is crushed by a tree cut down by a lumberjack with a chainsaw who get's a beehive stuck on his head, accidentally cuts of his own arms with the chainsaw and is impaled on a deer's antlers. The Cherubs ask Lyle to stop looking, but he tells them he can't and that he has never wanted to die more than he does now.

The Cherubs then take Lyle to a shopping mall during the Christmas season, where a large crowd of children is gathered around a mall Santa, intending to show him the joy of childhood wonderment. Lyle calls the children disease-ridden vermin but finds their untainted innocence inspiring, and he thanks the Cherubs for showing this to him. Blitzo then calls out to Lyle before ruining the Christmas spirit for the children by ripping the suit off the Mall Santa, revealing him to be a pedophilic gnome that scares away all of the children and causes Lyle to cry.

It then cuts to an open field, which Lyle complains reeks of teenagers as the Cherubs say they want to remind him of life's greatest joy. Lyle asks them if it's money, but the Cherubs tell him love. Lyle says he has never been in love before but imagines it's pretty nice. The Cherubs tell him it's not too late to find love, but Blitzo cuts them off, using a megaphone to ask if anyone would want to sleep with Lyle, making everybody flee in their cars. The Cherubs then confront I.M.P, calling them cruel, saying they're trying to give hope to someone in need. Moxxie responds by asking if they're "so superior to (them) just because they want a selfish, greedy, authoritarian capitalist to keel over dead", which Blitzo says is making things too real, spraying Moxxie from a spray bottle marked "PISS".

The Cherubs attempt to demonstrate the wonders of art and music to Lyle at an opera house. Above the stage, Millie questions how they will manage to make this experience look bad, while Moxxie insists they can't do that since there’s nothing negative about opera. Blitzo argues that they should still disrupt the experience, and he grabs a spotlight, moving it around the stage and causing chaos for the opera singer, who runs around trying to stay in the light. Lyle remarks that the singer isn’t performing well, and as Blitzo frantically maneuvers the spotlight, it becomes dislodged and falls, tragically crushing the woman to death just as she sings her final note. This horrific event causes the Cherubs to snap, and they leave Lyle alone to confront the imps. An argument ensues, with the Cherubs aiming their magical crossbows at the imps. They question why the imps are preventing them from doing their jobs, to which Moxxie responds with the same inquiry. The Cherubs insist they are saving Lyle, whether he wants it or not. Blitzo interrupts, stating that someone wants Lyle dead. They had been paid in advance, and because Blitzo had already spent the money on a jewel-encrusted horse statuette named Mare Ajuana, Lyle must pay the price. The Cherubs chastise the imps, calling them “disgusting, loathsome beasts,” and refer to Hell as dirt that the deceased—or in Keenie's words, “shitty dead people”—walk upon. They accuse I.M.P of meddling with the living. Millie angrily points out that they are doing exactly the same thing and insults the Cherubs, which leads to a fight breaking out.

As Keenie grapples Millie off the catwalk, Blitzo and Moxxie run away from the other Cherubs. Moxxie dives off the catwalk, grabs a rope and swings out over the stage, shooting a sandbag so it falls onto Keenie, fighting Millie and then catching her. Millie and Moxxie then begin spinning around the stage, firing wildly as they passionately kiss. As Lyle watches the two groups of otherworldly beings fighting over him, Lyles realize he only has one life, and if they're fighting over it, then it's undoubtedly one worth living. He also realizes since he's rich, he can "buy all the things".

The fight between Blitzo and Cletus causes a row of stage lights to fall onto and partially destroy the stage, launching a piano into the air that lands on and kills Lyle. Moxxie points out that the Cherubs did their job for them, and Cletus tells them this isn't over as the Cherubs attempt to return to Heaven. However, they are confused when they are mysteriously repelled. Another portal opens, and another group of Cherubs emerges. Among them is their snarky boss

, who passive-aggressively tells them they are banished from returning to Heaven because their actions proved to be the direct cause of death to a human. To add insult to injury, there isn't anything they can do to be forgiven. C.H.E.R.U.B attempts to blame I.M.P, but they've already left the theater. As the second group of Cherubs returns to Heaven, Cletus bawls his eyes out at the prospect of being left behind in the mortal world forever.

In their meeting room, Blitzo tells I.M.P they failed because the Cherubs killed Lyle and Lyle is probably in Heaven, and they have to figure out how to break the news to Loopty. Moxxie asks how Blitzo plans to do it, and as he looks out through the hole Loopty made entering their office, he tells him that he sent a text, figuring it'll make him less angry. Loopty enters the meeting room, breaking through the wall again and narrowly missing Moxxie, who scrambles to the other end. Blitzo apologizes to him for failing when the wall breaks a second time, crushing Moxxie, and Lyle enters the room. Lyle explains his experimenting on the poor meant he was going to Hell anyway, and Loopty reveals the two have reconciled. As Lyle asks what two expert robotic inventors will do in Hell, Wally Wackford breaks through the ceiling, asking if he heard someone say "inventors". Blitzo asks everyone to stop breaking the walls because Moxxie will have to fix them before it cuts to him beneath a pile of rubble, frothing at the mouth. Blitzo complains about his day, which Wackford makes a bad joke about, angering him enough to tell everyone to leave his office.

This episode is shown to take place sometime in December, as evident by the Mall Santa and the flyer by the wall advertising a Christmas Sale at 25% off.

Two running gags in this episode involve Moxxie getting repeatedly crushed by debris, as well as Blitzo not remembering Loopty's name.

The episode is dedicated in loving memory of Adriana Margarita Rodriguez Hernandez, the mother of animator Adolfo Rodriguez, who worked on this episode.

According to FX animator, Andrew Statler, the reason for the captions blocking the faces of the suicidal victims as well as their blood splatters being censored in the "C.H.E.R.U.B Jingle" musical sequence was due to YouTube's restrictions on blood and gore violence.

Despite this, the sequences where Lyle witnesses the violence and cruelty of nature and the massacre at the opera house were not censored.

In the Prime Video version of the episode, the blood and gore from the "C.H.E.R.U.B Jingle" musical sequence are uncensored.

Interestingly enough, in the scene where the de-aging machine first appeared, the switch appeared to be at the correct setting right before they entered the machine. However, the lever changed position to forward when it is seen it again. It is unclear whether this is a continuity error or intentional sabotage.

The ending originally had everyone awkwardly laughing at Wally's joke, but there was a problem with the audio when Vivziepop sent it to the animator Logan Niblock, who animated it all awkward and silent, leading Vivziepop to decide to leave it as is

and felt it was funnier that Wally would laugh at his own joke.
