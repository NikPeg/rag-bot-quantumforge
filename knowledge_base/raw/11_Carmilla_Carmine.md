Carmilla Carmine is a tall humanoid demon with an hourglass figure, light grey skin, and very long, thick white hair with a dark grey streak in her bangs. Her hair is usually kept into a pair of long and large

-like horns by thin, ballerina-like black ribbons, but when she lets her hair down and loose, it is calf-length and voluminous.

Her mouth has black lips and noticeable small fangs inside, and she has a marking colored in a darker shade of her grey skin tone resembling a mask that envelopes her eyes, which themselves have white irises, bright red sclera and black slit pupils. She has thin arms and big hands that reaches from her deltoids down, with her hands being rather large in proportion to her body with black pointed nails. She also has bridged wrists, instead of the alignment of her arms narrowing at the wrists like humans and most demons.

Carmilla wears attire similar to a ballerina, which consists of a short-sleeved off-the-shoulder black dress with two small white buttons on the left side and a white stripe located down the front and at the rim of the spiked white skirt, which includes carmine-red lining. She also wears long white opera gloves, and bistre-black waist stockings covered by very long white ballet shoelaces.

Carmilla walks on the tips of her toes and feet in her white pointé shoes, which in "

Hello Rosie!

" are revealed to be made out of

angelic steel

. She also accessorizes with a set of black hoop earrings, despite lacking visible ear lobes.

In the flashback of the most recent extermination prior to the pilot, she is shown to be wearing a short-sleeved, v-neck, all-black, form-fitting battlesuit with white veins running through it that line her shoulders, chest, and small narrow waist. The suit consists of black pants that emphasize her long legs with her usual pointé shoes, a white belt with X-shaped straps tied around her hips, a white collar that lines her clavicle, and her long opera gauntlets are accompanied by white stripes that separate them from her deltoids.

In the pilot, Carmilla's appearance is nearly the same, only she has long and wavy tailbone-length hair in a loose style with smaller horns, her face marking is less detailed, her hands have longer and thinner fingers with long sharp red claws, and she instead wore a long-sleeved black bodysuit with a red belt.

Carmilla appears to be largely professional, though she can lose her temper, especially when her friends are insulted or her family is in danger.

claims that losing her temper is usually very unlike her, indicating that she is usually calm and level-headed.

Carmilla has a deep dedication to her family, which stems from her losing a loved one a long time ago. She loves and cares about her daughters, willing to cross any line to protect them, even if it means killing an

while knowing there will be disastrous consequences for it. She even cares about her fellow Overlord, Zestial, willing to protect him too.

Carmilla wants to avoid resorting to violence and avoid going to war, but at the same time, she'll do whatever it takes to protect her loved ones, even if a protective action goes against her non-violence policies, such as her deal with Vox. She refuses to help Vaggi join Charlie's war against the Exorcist angels due to the fallout threatening her daughters' safety, but she does have a sense of pride when she demonstrates to Vaggi the weakness in the Exorcist angel's fighting style as well as motivates Vaggi to "

Her name "Carmilla" means "garden" or "orchard", while "Carmine" is a shade of red and a red pigment.

Her last name may also be a rhyme or play on the term "carbine" (car-byne) meaning a short rifle, which matches well with her company in scavenging, recycling, and distributing angelic steel.

The garden meaning of the name Carmilla may also reference the famous axiom "It's better to be a warrior in a garden, than a gardener in a war" attributed to legendary Japanese swordsman/kensei Miyamoto Musashi.
