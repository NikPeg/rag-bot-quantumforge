The episode opens on a concert in the

hosted by the Sin of Avarice - the unsympathetic and egotistical Mammon. The venue is sold out and among the crowd are a teenage

. When Mammon bombastically unveils himself, he announces a clown pageant, the winner of which will become the new face of his clown-themed brand (which he not-so-secretly just wants to use as an exploitative sales gimmick). In the crowd, Fizz is loving everything about it while Spike remains skeptical. Mammon then lets his clowns loose on the audience.

When the event ends and Spike and Fizz are walking home, Fizz tells Spike that he'd like nothing more than to win that pageant and work with Mammon. However, Spike states it's pointless considering the fame and fortune they've already netted from

their circus

and it's not worth becoming Mammon's creepy mascot. Fizz elaborates that it's not about the fame and fortune, but rather the chance to work alongside his lifelong idol and goes as far as to label him as an inspiration. Simply, Spike tells him Mammon is definitely something, hiding the truth of how he doesn't like him and goes on to ask if all the crazy antics that come with Mammon's endorsement are worth their life savings. When Fizz says it's totally worth it, a stranger is lurking in the shadows. He asks Spike if he could actually do it with hard work and dedication and Spike tells him he's the only hellion who would ever be the right fit for all things "clown", if anyone. Just then, the two of them are intercepted by

Arick Burnz

, an obsessive fan of their circus who creeps out Fizz to no end and tries pitching his own idea for an act in their performance involving a romantic Ballroom Dance. Hostilities escalate between him and Spike, who recognizes him as a creepy stalker he regularly sees snooping around their dressing rooms, and the two of them leave in huff. Arick melodramatically and arrogantly denounces them out of spite for turning their back on him, mostly due to his extremely uncomfortable demeanor. Upon hearing his harsh words, Fizz questions if his acts are just trash and Spike tells him to not dwell on the opinions of one obsessive asshole. Despite this, Fizz still feels vulnerable, claiming that if he wants to succeed, he needs to be perfect.

After having won a clown pageant, Fizz's obsessive training and vigorous preparation has paid off and he becomes the face of Mammon's clown brand, and is part of an advertisement to sell plush toys and robotic simulacrums in his image for $69.99.

In present day at

Azmod' Tower

, Fizz is scrambling to put on his foundation that conceals an injury on his face in preparation for his 10th Clown Pageant when

walks in. However, he doesn't understand what Fizz's worries are all about, claiming that Mammon's clown pageant is stupid. But all Fizz can say is he wants to be perfect for Mammon and to make him proud. As a result, Azmod tells him there's no such thing as perfect and he tries to convince him maybe it's time for him to retire from the pageant. Better yet, he suggests taking a vacation someplace where he doesn't have to fend off creepy fans. However, stating that he's always had to fend off creepy fans even before the simulacrums, Fizz claims this is just something that he has to do. Azmod explains that he doesn't like the idea of Fizz having to fend off fans, much less designing sex toys in Fizz's likeness for Mammon. However, Fizz sticks with his earlier statement that working for Mammon is a big deal to him, especially since he's been his idol since he was five years old, claiming that he can't let him down by not competing. Azmod tells Fizz Mammon is not worth working for, claiming that he's known Mammon since the beginning of

existence and he's always sucked and as much as he loves Fizz, he doesn't want him doing the clown schtick for someone's approval. Fizz acknowledges Azmod and he tells him that this pageant is for him and he just doesn't want to lose.

, Spike is watching TV and eating cereal in his apartment when he gets a call from Azmod. The two exchange hellos and Azmod explains Fizz's obsession with the annual clown pageant, hoping Spike could help him in explaining to him that it's a waste of time. But Spike isn't worried since Fizz is undefeated until Azmod tells him that Fizz doesn't listen when he tries telling him that Mammon is a selfish, manipulative piece of shit. What he's hoping for is, because of their history and newly rekindled friendship, Spike could talk some sense into him. This makes Spike agree to come to the Clown Pageant.

The trio arrive via limousine at Mammon's theater and Fizz asks why Spike is accompanying them. Azmod tells him because the secrecy of their romance doesn't allow the two of them to be seen together, Spike has to accompany him for security detail, citing how he was able to keep him safe when were kidnapped by

. Azmod departs and Fizz is greeted by Mammon, who tells of his stiff competition. When Spike sees Mammon ridiculing Fizz over "minor details" which could prove detrimental to his sales image, Spike doesn't hesitate to denounce one of Mammon's own performances. He tells Fizz to keep smiling and he departs, leaving Spike to not hesitate to say he does not like him at all. Fizz tells Spike while he still doesn't know the real reason Azmod brought him to the venue, he orders him not to talk back to his boss. When Spike questions why he needs this gig, pointing out his apparent affair with the Azmod, Fizz says he just needs it and proceeds with smiling and waving to his fans on the red carpet. However, tensions arise when his competition rolls in:

Glitz and Glam

, twin sisters with sharp claws and sharper tongues. Fizz tries to exchange pleasantries with them, but they just denounce him as a fugly imp and are looking forward to crushing anything which dares interfere with their victory. This makes Spike encourage Fizz to bring his A-Game for the sole purpose of spiting them.

Fizz and the twins compete in the opening act while

intense music

plays and they tie for 666 on the score board. Afterwards, Mammon hosts the admission for meet-&-greets with the stars and refuses to allow Fizz to skip it when he doesn't feel comfortable with meeting the fans at this time. Fizz tries to stand up for himself, but Mammon selfishly pressures him into doing it for the sake of the show. Spike tries to get him to explain why he lets Mammon walk all over him, but Fizz still refuses to open up to him. He greets the fans and marches past his discomfort. When he thanks them all for showing up, he is greeted by a young imp boy who tells him in sign language how he's a big fan. Fizz wholesomely greets him on his level and tells everyone to be excited for the finale. Unfortunately, that's when Arick returns and denounces his career in front of everybody, still embittered by being turned away all those years ago. Fizz has his back against the wall when he menacingly approaches him and Spike swoops in, ready to blow him to kingdom come if he doesn't leave at once but just before he can, Fizz is overtaken by anxiety and Spike settles for hitting him away. Arick leaves and swears revenge, Mammon appears, inquiring on what just happened. Fizz feels pressured into telling him everything's fine and Mammon tells him in light of whatever this is, the sisters will go on first to give Fizz time to prepare. Spike questions him as to why he's really doing the pageant again, repeating Azmod' earlier claim of clown pageant being stupid. All Fizz tells him is this job is all that matters and he'll lose everything otherwise. Spike denounces Mammon as a trash fire using Fizz for everything, but Fizz defends him and he states that it's all part of the process to make him perfect, leaving Spike questioning as to what he'd need to be perfect for.

The twins go on and their

performance

knocks the audience dead. Watching them stresses Fizz to the point of making him flee for his dressing room. When he looks in the mirror, he sees that he's sweating so much, his foundation is running and exposing his scar. Seeing the amount of emotional trouble Fizz is in, Azmod risks exposure to appear in his dressing room to check on him. Fizz insists he's fine when Azmod can clearly see he's not. He tries getting through to Fizz once again, but he is still stubborn and he claims he needs to prove himself by winning. That's when Azmod explains Fizz only acts like he needs to be this perfect clown because Mammon is perpetually forcing this image onto him. Then, Fizz confesses he has his perfect life and Azmod because of Mammon and needs to win to prove he's still worthy. As a result of this, Azmod asks how Fizz could possibly lose him and Fizz tearfully explains Azmod is only Fizz's lover for what he is at his best. Fizz finally reaches his breaking point when he yanks off his hat and shows Azmod what's left of his horns and his scar-ridden face, denouncing himself as a barely adequate partner or mascot for any king of sin when

is who he really is. He believes he owes everything to him because without Mammon, he's nothing. Finally knowing the truth, Azmod explains to the vulnerable Fizz Mammon didn't do anything to make Fizz the great success in life. He did because he was always the inspiration Azmod fell in love with, and he doesn't hesitate to tell him he is loved. Azmod finishes his heartfelt statement by telling Fizz he's a way better performer than Mammon ever was - not something Fizz could trust in himself, until now. Afterwards, the two of them share a hug and Fizz is finally ready to rock the show, knowing that his Azmod will still be waiting for him, whether he wins it or not. After a

heartfelt duet

, they share a kiss, until they are interrupted by Arick, who returns with a dagger. Thankfully, Spike is there to blow his head off and he sees that the two of them really are an item and then he sarcastically congratulates them on being "fucking hypocrites".

With the audience losing hope in seeing Fizz's performance, Mammon addresses them when the stage lights up and he takes his seat with his Fizz simulacrum harem waiting for him. Fizz performs a heartfelt

telling the story of the abuse he's dealt with all his life and how he's ready to end this chapter with a bang. His performance goes off like fireworks and when it's all over, he finally quits in front of everybody and stands up to Mammon. He tries to walk out on him, before he transforms into his true form. Seeing Fizz in danger, Azmod transforms too and he prepares for battle. Mammon tries to blackmail Azmod into standing down by threatening to reveal his romance with Fizz, but instead Azmod announces it to the entire audience himself. However, instead of laughing and mocking them, the fans instead fangirl and they already knew they were in love and publicated it everywhere, much to Mammon's shock and confusion. Finally rendered powerless, Mammon can do nothing except menacingly telling Azmod he's going to regret revealing his deepest darkest secret and disappears, releasing an explosion that brings the whole place down.

In the limousine ride home, Fizz and Azmod are cuddling, when Spike asks the two of them "Who tops?". Fizz groans in annoyance and Azmod smiles as the episode ends.

This theme has two elements that are used as a "leitmotif" for Mammon: The melody itself (which is a play on the theme from the opera "
