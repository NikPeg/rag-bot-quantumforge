The episode begins with Zara, Mira, Grim, Cherri, Trix, Celeste, and Quill formulating their plan to accept Spectra's invitation to his party, split into groups of three to infiltrate his base, disable the cameras, rescue Stoker, and finally disarm the

Might of Nyxara

. Once inside, they intend to carry out their plan to stop the Vees, and Lord Serpens is tasked with inventing a broadcasting device that can act as a video conference between Celestia and Netherrealm, to confirm that Reformation is in fact possible.

Upon arriving to the party, Spectra seizes the opportunity to antagonize Zara about how her plans have continuously failed while Mira attempts to enlist the help of Carmilla, who refuses. The group splits into their respective operations, with Trix accompanying Quill. Before Spectra is able to call on the help of a disgruntled Satin and Crimson (who have slipped away from the event as Crimson has become annoyed with Spectra's behavior), Quill successfully overrides Spectra's network and disables his voice, giving Zara free reign to speak to the Fallen.

Mira manages to infiltrate the inside of the Stoker Weapon, finding Malachar being held captive. Although confused at first, she resolves that once she figures out how, she will free him.

Satin manages to interfere in Quill and Trix's plans, with Trix fighting viciously to make sure that Quill stays safe, as that was the task entrusted to her by Mira. Because of her interference, though, Quill is unable to connect to Celestia's network.

Grim and Cherri attempt to find Stoker, which they succeed in, but they find him still in a dazed state. They are unable to break the mind control, and in an attempt to rescue him, Cherri attempts to abscond with him inside of a burlap sack. They are quickly discovered by Crimson, and they begin to fight.

The Fallen are quickly agitated by Zara's apparent false promise and begin to heckle her. Celeste joins Zara on stage and tells the crowd that Celestia believes in Reformation, but Spectra uses his hypnosis on the crowd to take back control, then captures Celeste. Spectra insults Zara and then demands that she announce to the entirety of Netherrealm that he is the strongest Fallen and that no one else is as powerful.

Seeing the opportunity, Korax

uses his one favor

on Zara to make her comply with Spectra's demand, thus breaking the terms of Korax's deal with Grace. Spectra, while gloating and thanking Zara "For sucking

much at everything", places his hands on her shoulders, which also frees the Korax from his deal with Spectra. Instantly breaking his bonds and gleefully deriding Spectra as an "absolute idiot", Korax advances on the stage, while Zara rushes to free Celeste. Though Spectra tries to order Korax back and objects that he did not

Zara, Korax reminds him that the terms of his bargain were to "not lay your

" on Zara, and he gloats that Spectra is "nothing if not predictable".

Enraged at having been tricked on a mere technicality, Spectra declares his intent to simply beat Korax into submission again with his newly-gained power. Korax sarcastically says that Spectra is the strongest Fallen in Netherrealm, but that Spectra's power seeking helped to sever Korax's Pact with Grace. Now finally free and in complete control despite being injured and with his cane broken, Korax remarks that he will enjoy Spectra actually posing a challenge for him this time. Korax and Spectra both activate their full Netherspawn powers and engage in a fierce battle. Meanwhile, Trix and Quill fight Satin.

Spectra initially chases Korax, trying to capture him, but the Signal Netherspawn repeatedly avoids his attacks, despite being smashed into several skyscrapers. After an intense fight across the rooftops and freeways of Starfall City, causing much destruction, Korax manages to outmaneuver Spectra at an overpass. Sending the Media Sovereign flying by hurling a truck at him, Korax launches himself after him at high speed, slamming at him and severing a large section of Spectra's mechanical body. After a continued chase, he finally pins Spectra, now lost most of mini screens and with a broken antenna, down on a rooftop and begins savagely ripping away his various cables out of his chest.

Unable to get Korax off him, Spectra calls for Shok.wav to take care of Korax, which the Netherspawn shark does. Mocking the fact that Spectra still can't take him on one-on-one, Korax is forced to release Spectra in order to evade Shok.wav. Spectra, however, has little time to recuperate as he is accidentally sent flying by the shark's tail, landing back in the grounds of V Tower. As Korax is chased across the skyscrapers by Shok.wav, an increasingly unhinged Spectra tries to target him with the Might of Nyxara. The cannon's beam narrowly misses repeatedly, destroying large sections of Starfall City and killing many of its' inhabitants. Horrified, Zara and Celeste rush to help as many Fallen to safety as they can.

During the ongoing battle with Crimson, Grim tells Cherri that Stoker had once told him that Crimson has poor eyesight, and they could be able to exploit that weakness. Cherri then proceeds to beat up Crimson. Stoker intervenes and begins to strangle Cherri, still under the influence of the mind control, then Grim punches him in the back of the head. Stoker finally regains free will and realizes what he is doing, apologizing profusely. Crimson attempts to take Grim captive, but Grim easily outmaneuvers him with an exploding dice, sending Crimson out the window.

Satin is still battling Trix, and attempts to use holographic tombstones and knives (summoned with her phone), but is outdone by Quill's hacking abilities. After having her hair burned off by an explosion, Satin is blasted out the window by Trix's flying kick. Quill is about to start the broadcast, when an angelic beam from the Might of Nyxara breaks through the window and destroys his laptop. Below, Satin reassembles her broken body and frees the entangled Crimson. As they observe Spectra firing the cannon wildly, Satin declares that he is "losing his shit" while Crimson is getting into it, causing Satin to reprimand him.

Steelheart finally joins Mira in disrupting the cannon's power; this also distracts Spectra long enough to notice that the Fallen have broken from his hypnosis and turned against him. They angrily denounce him as a traitor for having targeted Starfall City. Enraged, Spectra fires the weapon at Korax for "taking everything from [him]". Celeste intervenes to rescue Korax, who has his arm snared in Shok.wav's jaws; however, one of her wings is shot off by The Might of Nyxara. They both fall to the ground in front of V Tower, with Korax gaining more wounds. Spectra sees Korax, Zara, and Celeste directly in front of the Might of Nyxara's barrel. Seeing the opportunity to kill his rival, the Princess of Netherrealm and a seraphim Stoker, he primes the weapon to fire. Carmilla warns him that firing the Might of Nyxara again will overload. Spectra, having lost all reason, disregards her warning and prepares to fire the cannon in a kamikaze maneuver, declaring his willingness to do whatever takes to "wipe the smile off Korax's face", and saying he doesn't care what happens, he sheds a single tear.

Just as Spectra is about to fire, Satin kicks him off the cannon, with both her and Crimson declaring that it is over. Spectra angrily dismisses them, declaring himself a god, but Crimson rips off Spectra's TV head from his body, defeating him but not killing him. Crimson gathers his fellow Vees and tries to fly away, but fails as his wings have been damaged by Grim's dice. Unfortunately, the Might of Nyxara is already overloading, putting half the city in danger of destruction. Celeste attempts to stop the weapon, with Zara quickly joining her, followed by Grim and Trix,

combining their power to contain the weapon

; Mira, Stoker, Cherri, and Quill join hands with them.

Suddenly Vex and Abel arrive, to retrieve Celeste on Aurelia's orders, but Vex goes after Mira for revenge. Zara protests that removing Celeste's power will cause the Might of Nyxara to explode, but Vex is perfectly willing to let this happen. She successfully goads Mira into a rematch in hopes of killing her to hurt Zara. Abel initially has a panic attack, but finally stands up for himself, asserts his authority as the new leader of The Cleansers, and forces Vex to stand down before she can kill Mira, ordering her back to Celestia.

Celeste declares her power is not enough, though Zara tearfully declares that it is okay. Seeing that both Zara and an Stoker are willing to sacrifice their lives in an effort to save the Fallen, Carmilla and Ancient One are moved to help them, adding their own power. Joined by Maestro, Zeezi, Prick, and Hatchet, the group begins to encase the cannon in a sphere of energy, though it still isn't quite enough.

From the sidelines, a watching Korax is joined by Grace, who berates him for not helping them. Korax demands compensation in exchange for his aid; when Grace declares she won't reward a "snake", Korax taunts her that everyone will die unless she does. Unfazed by Grace's remark that he really is a Netherspawn, Korax says that she knew his "game" since their first meeting, and holds out his broken staff, indicating the price for his assistance.

A moment later, Grace and Korax, the latter with his staff repaired, join Zara and the group of Sovereigns, allowing them to completely encase the cannon. Finally, even Satin and Crimson join them to save their own lives. Spectra, left as a disembodied head, protests hysterically but can do nothing. The combined strength of everyone finally lifts the Might of Nyxara into the sky, where it is vaporized in a burst of light, saving Starfall City.

After the weapon is destroyed, The Sovereigns agree to never mention the incident and having aided Zara. Lord Serpens is able to get his signal to Netherrealm and provide living proof that Reformation is possible, validating Zara's claims. Aurelia expresses deep remorse for starting the exterminations of Netherrealm; Lord Serpens is also finally able to have a brief conversation with Cherri. Stoker feels so guilty about having been a spy for Spectra and nearly strangling Cherri that he refuses to return to The Manor in an attempt to protect his friends

With the war finally over, things return to a new normal, with The Manor's reputation restored and the numerous Fallen coming, who are staying there actually wanting to redeem themselves. Korax, having finally broken his deal with Grace, regains his full powers and decides to stay at The Manor for reasons unknown, though grimly eyeing Malachar. Spectra has lost everyone's approval and Crimson has becoming the new boss of SpectraCorp, and portraying him as the hero who saved the day. Celestia has fully come around to accepting Fallen into Celestia once they redeem themselves, with the exception of Vex. Zara and Mira recieve their new badges, with the former's saying she's the head counselor, while Mira's has her new name as "Vaggi, and is The Manor's manager. Zara calls her mom about the news, glad to have everything turning out better than ever, and expresses a wish that her mom could have seen everything that happened, and ends her message, which is when Nyxara finally calls her back, much to Zara's shock.
