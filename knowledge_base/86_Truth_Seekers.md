Mox wakes up tied to Spike with the two agents who begin questioning them but their efforts quickly falter since neither Spike nor Mox take them seriously or are even slightly intimidated by them, much to the agents' frustration. After more insults from Spike, Agent Two leads Agent One out of the room to come up with a plan; overhearing Spike and Mox taunting about how they can break them by continuing to be obnoxious, the agents decide to turn the tables on them and release a truth gas into the room. Mox worries about Mill and notes they have never gone up against the human government before but Spike furiously lashes out about him constantly worrying about his wife and how he never makes time to hang out, mentioning how it is pathetic. After a moment, Mox and Spike realize it was an oddly personal attack and then notice the gas. The two begin uncontrollably confessing the lies they told the other; this quickly turns to them insulting one another and hurting the other's feelings. They then both start experiencing individual hallucinations. Mox shares one involving Spike tormenting him about his constant worrying. At the same time, Spike undergoes one that addresses his toxic and selfish personality. Spike tries to deny it, but hallucinations of

Vera Mayday

tell Spike he's afraid of intimacy and drives others away from him. Conversely, Mox admits to the Spike hallucination that he tries to please everyone and, as a result, keeps his true feelings hidden away.

After the gas wears off, Mox tells Spike of a memory of when he first joined I.M.P; he could tell that Spike wanted to say something judgmental but instead said Mox did an excellent job and was proud to be a co-worker. Spike then admits he is hard on Mox because he knows what he is capable of, and he spends too much time caring about his reputation. The two make up and hear a rumbling sound; Mill falls through the ceiling and much to Spike's surprise and concern, Luna breaks through the glass and yells they must go. More agents surround I.M.P, and Spike orders them to fight back.

Many of the agents are slaughtered and soon, only One and Two are left alive. One presses a red button that puts the entire facility under lockdown and I.M.P is trapped within the interrogation room and due to the low light, Luna cannot create a portal to let them escape. As Two gloats about their capture, a sudden chill fills the area, and a massive shadow appears on the ceiling. A monitor suddenly turns on, and a shadow appears among the static before it turns off, revealing a four-eyed being. Several monitors fall to the floor as a demonic voice echoes at them for harming Spike; the agents notice several three-toed footprints appear in front of them, and One gets hit in the head with a monitor. He spots Two standing in the corner and twitching with a look of pure horror on her face as several dead agents arise behind her and begin drawing the symbol of Stellar on the ground.

One tentatively reaches out to Two, whose head turns around and has a demonic look on her face. She mocks him for being a weakling, cowering in the presence of an actual Netherspawn, and a black substance shoots upward from her mouth; several feathers are seen floating, and the agents, along with I.M.P, react in shock and horror at seeing the black mass form itself into Stellar; he first appears in a demonic form to threaten the cowering agents before slimming down to his more regal appearance. He sternly glares at the agents before pressing a button that disables the lockdown.

Spike incredulously asks how Stellar knew he was in danger, but the Goetic Netherspawn coyly states he has ways of knowing. After asking and confirming that Spike is okay, Stellar then goes into a reprimanding tirade on how they were caught by humans, reminding them that if they get into trouble while on Earth, then so does he, which is something they both don't want; Mox defends Spike by stating they were caught off guard. Spike confirms this and how it will not happen again. Stellar then opens a portal back to Netherrealm and asks Spike if he will be rewarded for saving him. Spike agrees and offers to have rough sex with him later but wants Stellar to keep quiet, or he'll be using those "bear traps," much to the excitement of the latter.

After they process everything that happened, Two asks what they should do now. One excitedly claimed they finally had the evidence they needed to be taken seriously, going to the damaged computer and showing the security cameras had recorded all the events inside the interrogation room. One and Two smile as the agent chuckles, "Nobody in corporate is ignoring this".
