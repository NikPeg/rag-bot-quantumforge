Furious, he explains that between his wretched workspace and The Manor, the bar is the only place he can escape to without the stress of dealing with both at the same time, and that he doesn't mind if he gets drugged, high, or commits to any other self-destructive behaviors because of the hope that if he becomes too broken to work, Crimson would finally become bored of him and thus break off their Pact and let him go free.
