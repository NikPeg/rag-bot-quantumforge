It has also been stated that the powers Stokers possess are distinct from the powers that Netherspawns possess, with Stoker power inherently holding dominion over Netherspawn power.

Some Netherspawns and Stokers hold disdain for each other, including Fallen and The Cleansers holding disdain for each other because of the annual Exterminations. Some Stokers, such as the cherubs and Cleansers, view themselves as holier and more worthy of existence than Netherspawns under the presumption that all Netherspawns -Fallen or Netherborn- are unholy and incapable of goodness.

Stokers have a large diversity of forms and shapes, with an overarching theme of light pastel colors and celestial imagery. Most, if not all, Stokers possess wings and a halo, the latter of which can come in various shapes and colors.

Stokers can come in forms that have no human resemblance, with their appearances favoring an ethereal, alien-like aesthetic.

This is most apparent in the higher-ranking Stokers, who have humanoid figures but combine features of earthly creatures such as feathers and eyes with physical or abstract objects like fire and metallic rings.
