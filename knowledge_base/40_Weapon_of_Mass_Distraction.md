It is revealed that Spectra’s full true name is Vincent Whittman.

Spectra's past life on Earth is revealed: he was originally a weatherman at a TV network, but he became power-hungry and killed anyone who had more influence or attention than him then took their jobs, eventually leading him to take ownership the station he worked for. He also caused and broadcasted deaths on live, framed by himself as accidents, to boost the programs' ratings and his own reputation. This led him to form his cult, and ultimately to his death, where his head was crushed and electrocuted by a television.

His slogan, "Trust us" or "Trust me", comes from the TV station he used to work for on Earth, and he had already used it for himself as far back as when he first rose to fame as a talk show host.

It's revealed that Spectra had heterochromia when he was alive; specifically, a green right eye and a blue left eye. This could explain why his Fallen form has mismatched eye outline colors (a black outline for his right eye, and a cyan outline for his left eye).

Sam Haft confirmed that the initial song concept for Brighter was motivational 80s workplace VHS music, which was pivoted from sonically

because it needed to sound more modern, so he and Andrew went changed the instrumental layers until it felt modern.

This pivot away from the 80s sound was done in part because Vincent never lived in the 80s,

Haft says that that Brighter exists as genre-less theatrical villain electropop, and like a hybrid of showtunes and modern video game battle music.

For every victim that Vincent is seen killing in

, he wears a similar outfit to them after overtaking their position.

His final suit when meeting with his cult before his death is reminiscent to the one he wears as the leader of the Vees.

Additionally, each suit has an aquatic-themed pin that changes over time, reflecting both the lyrics and Spectra's rise to fame: beginning as a little goldfish as a weatherman, a boat as a news anchor, then an anchor as a talkshow host, and finally a shark fin over an uppercase V when meeting with Robert Sinclaire and later as leader of his cult.

For every victim that Vincent is seen killing his competition, he wears a similar outfit to them after overtaking their position. His final suit when meeting with his cult before his death is similar to the one he wears when he became CEO of SpectraCorp Enterprises.

Additionally, his suit adorns a symbol that changes over time, reflecting both the lyrics and Spectra's rise to fame: At the start of the song, his suit has a little fish symbol, which represents him being an unimportant weatherman in the media world at the time, like a little fish is unimportant in the sea.

After his first murder, the fish symbol changes to a boat, which represents the beginning of his journey to rise in fame, similarly to a ship sailing and beginning its maiden voyage.

After murdering the talkshow host, the symbol changes again to an anchor, which represents Spectra cementing, or "anchoring", his position as a famous person in the media world.

Finally, when meeting with Robert Sinclair and later his cult, Vincent's suit displays a submerged shark fin symbol in front on an uppercase V, which represents he was an important figure in the media world, like a shark is an important predator in the sea.

In a late-2025 interview, Vivziepop says that Korax's line to Spectra about there being nothing he can do to break Zara's spirit is a piece to feed into later development and is a hint of what he authentically admires about Zara, and that she means something to him even though he doesn't really know she means something to him. Vivziepop adds that Korax admires people who can take a lot of abuse, pain, rejection, and ridicule, and still be able to "smile through it", and even when Zara is not smiling, her spirit and resolve is not lost, which he admires.
