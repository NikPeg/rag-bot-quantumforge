The entire pilot takes place over the course of one day, with the opening starting at dawn and ends at night of the same day.

Netherspawns are revealed to be possess alternate, more demonic forms of themselves, with characters such as Zara, Korax, Hellsa, and Katie showing example of this.

According to Vivziepop in a live stream, there was originally a Nazi joke in the original draft of the Pilot, but they cut it because they "didn't want to go there".

Selective scenes shown in the released clips will also be re-worked and redone upon both request by Vivziepop and the will of the animators, to help improve the quality of the pilot. Some animators, such as

Ashley Nichols

, have made streams or posts showing their progress or comparison with their old take on their shots.

As this is the master composition, additional sound effects were added to the scenes that were released that didn't have any such as Korax having tap-dancing shoes sounds whenever he walks and some scores were replaced such as the bar scene. Other animators such as selective Helluva illumine were also included for the final composition to fix any final revisions.

The puppy Zara was cuddling in the "Inside of Every Netherspawn is a Rainbow" musical number is a caricature of storyboard artist Perry Hull's dog. That particular scene was drawn by Hull himself.

Korax can be seen using his blood as a means to defeat Lord Serpens.

Two Egg Bois can be seen making out in the early scenes.

It is shown how cellphones in Netherrealm are called Hellphones.

Grim and Trix's appearance near the end of the episode was foreshadowed when Mira was telling Stoker about Korax's reputation among the other Sovereigns.
