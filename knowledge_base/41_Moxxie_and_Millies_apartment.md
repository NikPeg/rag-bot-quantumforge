Beside the door is Mox and Mill's wedding photo above a small drawer table with eye flowers in a green vase and two candles. The living room has a couch with rips and several daggers in it, a low coffee table on a carpet, and a small flat screen television. The living room wall has three playbills, three daggers, and a pipe running up to the roof.

Mox and Mill's bedroom walls have several musical-related items. The bed has a large bedhead with jewels and a heart-like design on the front, and several daggers stabbed in it. On Mox's side of the bed is a bedside table with an alarm clock. On Mill's side of the bed is what appears to be a crooked lamp, and a dagger stabbed into a bedside table.
