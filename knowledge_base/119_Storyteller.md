Primus then asks what is she going to do about it, Vex answers that she is

going to kill every last one of them

Aurelia decides to cut Celestia off from all other realms to prevent any possible war from breaking out, with any new Essences to be put under security checks for safety, and no one is to leave without permission. She and six other Stokers then combine their power to put a force field around Celestia, keeping the realm under lock down until further notice. Lord Serpens asks Celeste if this is good or bad, but she is unsure, and he then asks is Zara should know, reminding her that she has to tell Zara about his Reformation and the lockdown. She leaves before Lord Serpens can ask to see his friends, much to his sadness, while Celestia is completely closed off as the force field completes itself.

Lord Serpens ends up sulking on his new bed, with the Divine-made Egg Boiz surrounding him for comfort.
