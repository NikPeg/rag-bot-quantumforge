Zara then reintroduces Lord Serpens to Korax, who says he definitely remembers Lord Serpens now that he ruined his coat. Zara gets Lord Serpens to apologize to Korax, the former also returning the piece of fabric that he took off of Korax’s coat. Korax says that "not many people have managed to take even this much off" of him and it must have meant a lot to Lord Serpens, before burning it in front of him.

Later, Zara, Mira, Stoker and Lord Serpens are sitting in the parlor together, and Zara is getting them to play a rhyming game as an icebreaker. Zara and Lord Serpens each take turns, but Stoker refuses, calling it stupid. Mira tells him that he better get used to it, because it was going to be his whole day. After this, Stoker and Lord Serpens perform a skit while Zara, Mira, Korax, Grim and Trix watch them. Silk Shadow performs in complete deadpan, not liking the script, while Lord Serpens puts more effort into his acting. Zara congratulates Lord Serpens, saying he would soon be redeemed if he kept going at the rate he was. Stoker, looking sad, announces he's going to bed. Going to his room, he lies on his bed and listens to his voicemails from Crimson, which alternate between him being nice and him being threatening, until Crimson's voice echoes through the room, saying that Stoker can't change and "addict trash" like him can't be redeemed.

Saddened by this, Stoker goes downstairs to get a drink. While he's there, he notices Lord Serpens and follows him into another room, where he sees him putting a SpectraCorp camera on a shelf. Stoker confronts him and accuses him of working for the Vees. When Lord Serpens denies this and calls him a "Whorebug", Stoker attacks him, but Lord Serpens gets him off using hypnosis to stun him. Stoker quickly shakes it off, though. When Zara and Mira come into the room, Stoker tells them that Lord Serpens was a spy for the Vees and shows them the camera as proof. Pentious, panicking, attempts to contact Spectra through a watch that the Vees gave him. Spectra laughs at him for getting caught so quickly, before saying that if The Manor's residents didn't kill him, he should kill himself.
