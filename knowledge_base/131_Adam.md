Primus was an Stoker with a human appearance. Most of the time, he wore a mask that appeared similar to other Cleansers, though with normal-looking eyes and golden facial expressions. The mask also held a pair of horns similar to an Cleanser, albeit longer, smoother, and with a golden ornamental attachment on the tips.

Under his mask, he is revealed to be a fair-skinned Stoker with golden-yellow eyes that have dark bags underneath, a stubble-beard on his chin, and slightly messy, short cedar-brown colored hair.

He also had a pair of large and golden wings on his back. In contrast to other Stokers that have appeared thus far, Primus's halo was bright gold in appearance, and similar to The Cleansers, has two spikes pointing up and down from each other, though these spikes are located at the front of his halo.

He wore a white and golden cloak that appears to have a large 'A' symbol emblazoned on the front, and had gold-tipped spikes on the back of his collar. His visible hands were black in appearance.

In "The Show Must Go On", Malachar mentions that Primus had "really let himself go", implying Primus had become overweight. Primus's entire body gives off a white glow throughout his fight with Malachar until he collapses from being stabbed.

Primus appeared very egotistical and unsympathetic. As the leader of his

army, he took his stance as The Cleansers' head figure very seriously, sending antagonistic threats towards Netherspawns and anyone for standing in their way or whoever disobeyed his command, as he did so with

for refusing to kill a Fallen, cutting her wings, and leaving her in Netherrealm.

He also displayed traits of extreme narcissism and chauvinism, believing himself the perfect man due to being the first and claiming he had never made a mistake in his life, since he was the first man to be born on Earth, and he claimed that all the male bloodline came from him and brag about how big and great his dick is. His narcissism was so great that, upon his defeat, he refused to admit how far he had fallen and expressed a god-like complex by proclaiming his enemies should be grateful for him having fathered the beginning of humanity while demanding they worship him for his deeds.

Primus did not appear to be aware that his egotistical and depraved behavior towards Nyxara was the cause of her leaving him. When meeting

Despite this, he seemed to have a good relationship with Vex, whom he seemed to have in very high esteem, even though he was her superior.

Despite his obliviousness, Primus did show moments of common sense and self-awareness, such as when the death of an Cleanser was discovered, he knew that if they decided to kill the Fallen now after just one week after the most recent Purge, then Netherspawns would catch on to how Cleansers can be killed. Primus may have enjoyed playing music, specifically with a guitar, as he outright claimed to be in a band. He also had a poor view of radio and stereos, telling Korax those already belong to the past, considering them to be old and useless.

Primus was also quite cruel and sadistic, openly admitting in front of Zara that he enjoyed killing Netherspawns, calling it entertainment, and tended to announce how many killed in The Purge. He also dismissed Zara's project of Reformation not because he didn't believe in Fallen being redeemed, because if she succeeded with that project he wouldn't be able to continue killing Netherspawns. His eagerness to kill Netherspawns and get his revenge was so big that he advanced the date of the next Purge to six months instead of a year.

Despite his sadism and cruelty, Primus was capable of recognizing and praising the abilities of others, when he reunited with Vaggi he admitted she was one of the best Cleansers he had under his command. He also genuinely commended Vex for killing 275 Netherspawns during the most recent Purge, and congratulated her with a fist bump.
