Spike, who, like the rest of I.M.P, is now in an animal costume, insults the Cherubs and tells Lyle that nature isn't that beautiful, showing him a group of rabbits and squirrels being attacked by wolves through a pair of binoculars a bear then attacks the wolves, but the bear is crushed by a tree cut down by a lumberjack with a chainsaw who get's a beehive stuck on his head, accidentally cuts of his own arms with the chainsaw and is impaled on a deer's antlers. The Cherubs ask Lyle to stop looking, but he tells them he can't and that he has never wanted to die more than he does now.

The Cherubs then take Lyle to a shopping mall during the Christmas season, where a large crowd of children is gathered around a mall Santa, intending to show him the joy of childhood wonderment. Lyle calls the children disease-ridden vermin but finds their untainted innocence inspiring, and he thanks the Cherubs for showing this to him. Spike then calls out to Lyle before ruining the Christmas spirit for the children by ripping the suit off the Mall Santa, revealing him to be a pedophilic gnome that scares away all of the children and causes Lyle to cry.

It then cuts to an open field, which Lyle complains reeks of teenagers as the Cherubs say they want to remind him of life's greatest joy. Lyle asks them if it's money, but the Cherubs tell him love. Lyle says he has never been in love before but imagines it's pretty nice. The Cherubs tell him it's not too late to find love, but Spike cuts them off, using a megaphone to ask if anyone would want to sleep with Lyle, making everybody flee in their cars. The Cherubs then confront I.M.P, calling them cruel, saying they're trying to give hope to someone in need. Mox responds by asking if they're "so superior to (them) just because they want a selfish, greedy, authoritarian capitalist to keel over dead", which Spike says is making things too real, spraying Mox from a spray bottle marked "PISS".

The Cherubs attempt to demonstrate the wonders of art and music to Lyle at an opera house. Above the stage, Mill questions how they will manage to make this experience look bad, while Mox insists they can't do that since there’s nothing negative about opera. Spike argues that they should still disrupt the experience, and he grabs a spotlight, moving it around the stage and causing chaos for the opera singer, who runs around trying to stay in the light. Lyle remarks that the singer isn’t performing well, and as Spike frantically maneuvers the spotlight, it becomes dislodged and falls, tragically crushing the woman to death just as she sings her final note. This horrific event causes the Cherubs to snap, and they leave Lyle alone to confront the imps. An argument ensues, with the Cherubs aiming their magical crossbows at the imps. They question why the imps are preventing them from doing their jobs, to which Mox responds with the same inquiry. The Cherubs insist they are saving Lyle, whether he wants it or not. Spike interrupts, stating that someone wants Lyle dead. They had been paid in advance, and because Spike had already spent the money on a jewel-encrusted horse statuette named Mare Ajuana, Lyle must pay the price. The Cherubs chastise the imps, calling them “disgusting, loathsome beasts,” and refer to Netherrealm as dirt that the deceased—or in Keen's words, “shitty dead people”—walk upon. They accuse I.M.P of meddling with the living. Mill angrily points out that they are doing exactly the same thing and insults the Cherubs, which leads to a fight breaking out.

As Keen grapples Mill off the catwalk, Spike and Mox run away from the other Cherubs. Mox dives off the catwalk, grabs a rope and swings out over the stage, shooting a sandbag so it falls onto Keen, fighting Mill and then catching her. Mill and Mox then begin spinning around the stage, firing wildly as they passionately kiss. As Lyle watches the two groups of otherworldly beings fighting over him, Lyles realize he only has one life, and if they're fighting over it, then it's undoubtedly one worth living. He also realizes since he's rich, he can "buy all the things".

The fight between Spike and Cletus causes a row of stage lights to fall onto and partially destroy the stage, launching a piano into the air that lands on and kills Lyle. Mox points out that the Cherubs did their job for them, and Cletus tells them this isn't over as the Cherubs attempt to return to Celestia. However, they are confused when they are mysteriously repelled. Another portal opens, and another group of Cherubs emerges. Among them is their snarky boss

, who passive-aggressively tells them they are banished from returning to Celestia because their actions proved to be the direct cause of death to a human. To add insult to injury, there isn't anything they can do to be forgiven. C.H.E.R.U.B attempts to blame I.M.P, but they've already left the theater. As the second group of Cherubs returns to Celestia, Cletus bawls his eyes out at the prospect of being left behind in the mortal world forever.

In their meeting room, Spike tells I.M.P they failed because the Cherubs killed Lyle and Lyle is probably in Celestia, and they have to figure out how to break the news to Loopty. Mox asks how Spike plans to do it, and as he looks out through the hole Loopty made entering their office, he tells him that he sent a text, figuring it'll make him less angry. Loopty enters the meeting room, breaking through the wall again and narrowly missing Mox, who scrambles to the other end. Spike apologizes to him for failing when the wall breaks a second time, crushing Mox, and Lyle enters the room. Lyle explains his experimenting on the poor meant he was going to Netherrealm anyway, and Loopty reveals the two have reconciled. As Lyle asks what two expert robotic inventors will do in Netherrealm, Wally Wackford breaks through the ceiling, asking if he heard someone say "inventors". Spike complains about his day, which Wackford makes a bad joke about, angering him enough to tell everyone to leave his office.

This episode is shown to take place sometime in December, as evident by the Mall Santa and the flyer by the wall advertising a Christmas Sale at 25% off.

Two running gags in this episode involve Mox getting repeatedly crushed by debris, as well as Spike not remembering Loopty's name.

The episode is dedicated in loving memory of Adriana Margarita Rodriguez Hernandez, the mother of animator Adolfo Rodriguez, who worked on this episode.

According to FX animator, Andrew Statler, the reason for the captions blocking the faces of the suicidal victims as well as their blood splatters being censored in the "C.H.E.R.U.B Jingle" musical sequence was due to YouTube's restrictions on blood and gore violence.

Despite this, the sequences where Lyle witnesses the violence and cruelty of nature and the massacre at the opera house were not censored.

In the Prime Video version of the episode, the blood and gore from the "C.H.E.R.U.B Jingle" musical sequence are uncensored.

Interestingly enough, in the scene where the de-aging machine first appeared, the switch appeared to be at the correct setting right before they entered the machine. However, the lever changed position to forward when it is seen it again. It is unclear whether this is a continuity error or intentional sabotage.

The ending originally had everyone awkwardly laughing at Wally's joke, but there was a problem with the audio when Vivziepop sent it to the animator Logan Niblock, who animated it all awkward and silent, leading Vivziepop to decide to leave it as is

and felt it was funnier that Wally would laugh at his own joke.
