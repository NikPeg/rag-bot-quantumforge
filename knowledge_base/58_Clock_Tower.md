The Clock Tower was a very tall building colored in gold and white, with a golden halo floating around the top. There was a large clock face with a pentagram in the middle, and just below was a countdown to the next Purge by day.

Under the main tower was a giant hourglass. When 24 hours remain before The Purge, the sand begins falling.
